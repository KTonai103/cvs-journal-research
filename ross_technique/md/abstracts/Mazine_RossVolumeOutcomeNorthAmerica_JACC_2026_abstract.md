## Discussion

Herein, we report an analysis of the utilization trends and early outcomes of the Ross procedure in North America using data extracted from the STS ACSD over a 16-year period from 2008 to 2023. Our findings reveal that the total number of Ross procedures has significantly increased in recent years, accounting for a growing proportion of all isolated AVRs in younger patients. ## Conclusions

This study documents a resurgence in the use of the Ross procedure across North America. Consistent with other complex cardiac operations, we observed an inverse association between procedural volume and early outcomes. Operative mortality was higher in lower-volume centers and among lower-volume surgeons.
