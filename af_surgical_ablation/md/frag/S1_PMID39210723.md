## 4. 本文（§7.2.7 / §7.2.8 / §6.6 / §9.6）の実質内容と数値

### 4.1 §7.2.8 AF ablation during cardiac surgery — 本文の要点

- 術式の位置づけ（原文逐語）:
  > "**The best validated method of surgical ablation is the Maze procedure, consisting of a pattern of transmural lesions including PVI, with subsequent modifications using bipolar radiofrequency and/or cryothermy ablation with LAA amputation**"
  （文献 678–681 = Damiano Cox-Maze IV JTCVS 2011 / Cox JL Semin Thorac Cardiovasc Surg 2000 / Melby Ann Surg 2006「ablation technology and lesion set on procedural efficacy」/ **STS 2017 clinical practice guidelines for the surgical treatment of AF, Badhwar Ann Thorac Surg 2017**）
  → **lesion set の具体（box lesion, biatrial vs left-sided, cavotricuspid isthmus 等）についての記載は本文に一切なし**。"transmural lesions including PVI" と "LAA amputation" 以外の技術的規定はない。
- AF は心臓手術患者のリスク因子（原文逐語）:
  > "Atrial fibrillation is a significant risk factor for early mortality, late mortality, and stroke in patients referred for cardiac surgery."（文献 675–677）
- 有効性・安全性（原文逐語）:
  > "A number of RCTs have shown that surgical AF ablation during cardiac surgery **increases freedom from arrhythmia recurrence**.683–688 Performing surgical AF ablation, mainly targeting those patients needing mitral valve surgery, **is not associated with increased morbidity or mortality**.678,683–685"
  → **本文に freedom from AF の具体的な数値（%・HR・95%CI）は一切示されていない。**
- 観察研究の位置づけ（原文逐語）:
  > "Observational data, including large registries, have supported the potential value of surgical AF ablation,689–700 but **further RCTs are needed to evaluate which patients should be selected, and whether this approach contributes to the prevention of stroke, thromboembolism, and death**."
- チーム医療（原文逐語）:
  > "Education and training, close co-operation within a multidisciplinary team, and shared decision-making can improve the quality and outcomes of surgical ablation."（文献 682 = Ad N, Ann Thorac Surg 2013）

#### ★ 本章で唯一提示される定量データ = ペースメーカ植込み率

原文逐語:
> "In a systematic review of **22 RCTs (1726 patients)**, permanent pacemaker implantation rates were higher with surgical AF ablation than without concomitant AF surgery (**6.0% vs. 4.1%; RR, 1.69; 95% CI, 1.12–2.54**).701"

- 効果量: RR 1.69、95%CI 1.12–2.54、**P 値の記載なし**（原文に P は書かれていない）
- 分数の記載なし（**原文は % のみ**: 6.0% vs 4.1%）
- 出典 701 = Huffman MD et al. "Concomitant atrial fibrillation surgery for people undergoing cardiac surgery." Cochrane Database Syst Rev 2016;8:CD011814（**Cochrane レビューからの二次引用**）

続いて（原文逐語）:
> "Observational registry data from contemporary cohorts (**2011–2020**) suggest an overall pacemaker rate post-operatively of **2.1%** in patients selected for surgical AF ablation, with **no discernible impact of surgical ablation on the need for a pacemaker**, but higher rates in those needing multivalve surgery.702"

- 2.1% に 95%CI・P 値・分母の記載は**なし**（**原文は % のみ**）。出典 702 = Kowalewski M et al. Heart Rhythm 2022;19:1442–9（**二次引用**）。

#### 術中イメージング（Class I C の根拠）

原文逐語:
> "With a safety-first approach in mind, imaging is advised during surgical AF ablation to exclude thrombus and help to plan the surgical approach (**e.g. with TOE**), regardless of effective pre-procedural anticoagulant use."

（参考: §7.2.6 のカテーテルアブレーション文脈では、"The prevalence of left atrial thrombus was **1.3%** and **2.7%** in two meta-analyses of observational studies in patients planned for catheter ablation of AF on adequate OAC.649,650" と記載。CI・P 値の記載なし。これは surgical ablation の数値ではない点に注意。）

### 4.2 §7.2.7 Endoscopic and hybrid AF ablation — 本文の要点と数値

- 用語定義（原文逐語）:
  > "Minimally invasive surgical AF ablation can be performed via a **thoracoscopic approach or a subxiphoid approach. The term endoscopic covers both strategies.** Hybrid ablation approaches have been developed where **endoscopic epicardial ablation on the beating heart is performed in combination with endocardial catheter ablation, either in a simultaneous or sequential procedure.** The rationale for combining an endocardial with an epicardial approach is that a more effective **transmural** ablation strategy can be pursued."

- **発作性 AF（FAST 試験 長期成績）** 原文逐語:
  > "For paroxysmal AF, an endoscopic or hybrid ablation approach may be considered after a failed percutaneous catheter ablation strategy.668–670 Long-term follow-up of the FAST RCT (**mean of 7.0 years**), which included patients with paroxysmal and persistent AF, found arrhythmia recurrence was common but substantially lower with thoracoscopic ablation than catheter ablation: **34/61 patients (56%)** compared with **55/63 patients (87%)**, with **P < .001** for the comparison.669"
  - **分数がそのまま原文にある**: 34/61 (56%) vs 55/63 (87%)。**95%CI の記載なし**。追跡は "mean of 7.0 years"（median (IQR) の記載なし）。
  - 出典 669 = Castella M, Kotecha D, et al. "Thoracoscopic vs. catheter ablation for atrial fibrillation: long-term follow-up of the FAST randomized trial." Europace 2019;21:746–53。

- **持続性 AF / メタ解析** 原文逐語:
  > "For persistent AF, endoscopic or hybrid ablation approaches are suitable as a first procedure to maintain long-term sinus rhythm in selected patients.667–672 A meta-analysis of **three RCTs** confirmed a lower rate of atrial arrhythmia recurrence after thoracoscopic vs. catheter ablation (**incidence rate ratio, 0.55; 95% CI, 0.38–0.78**; with no heterogeneity between trials).669"
  - 効果量 IRR 0.55、95%CI 0.38–0.78、**P 値の記載なし**。
  - ※ 本文は引用番号 669（＝FAST long-term follow-up 論文）を付しているが、"meta-analysis of three RCTs" の帰属としてはやや不自然。**原文どおりに写した**が、引用番号の妥当性は一次資料側で要確認。

- **CASA-AF（否定的 RCT）** 原文逐語:
  > "An RCT with **12 month follow-up** published after the meta-analysis in patients with **long-standing persistent AF found no difference in arrhythmia freedom comparing thoracoscopic with catheter ablation**.673"
  - 数値（効果量・CI・P）の記載は**一切なし**。出典 673 = Haldar S et al. CASA-AF, Eur Heart J 2020;41:4471–80。

- **合併症** 原文逐語:
  > "Although overall morbidity and mortality of both techniques is low, **endoscopic and hybrid ablation have higher complication rates than catheter ablation, but similar long-term rates of the composite of mortality, MI, or stroke**.667,669"
  - 具体的な合併症率の数値は**記載なし**。

- **ハイブリッド（epicardial + endocardial）の近年 RCT** 原文逐語:
  > "More recent trials have assessed the efficacy and safety of the hybrid epicardial-plus-endocardial approach in persistent AF refractory to AAD therapy, including a **single-centre RCT670 and two multicentre RCTs.671,674** Across these trials, **hybrid ablation was consistently superior to catheter ablation alone for maintaining long-term sinus rhythm, without significant differences in major adverse events.** Notably, these studies were **typically performed in highly experienced centres** (see Supplementary data online, Additional Evidence Table S24)."
  - 効果量・CI・P 値は本文に**一切なし**（数値は supplementary Evidence Table S24 に格納、本 PDF 本文には無い）。
  - 670 = van der Heijden CAJ et al. JACC Clin Electrophysiol 2023;9:1013–23（single-centre RCT）
  - 671 = DeLurgio DB et al. **CONVERGE** trial, Circ Arrhythm Electrophysiol 2020;13:e009288
  - 674 = Doll N, Weimar T, et al. EClinicalMedicine 2023;61:102052（hybrid epicardial+endocardial vs endocardial の RCT）

### 4.3 §6.6 Surgical LAA occlusion — 本文の要点と数値

- **LAAOS III** 原文逐語:
  > "The Left Atrial Appendage Occlusion Study (**LAAOS III**) randomized **4811** patients with AF to undergo or not undergo LAAO at the time of cardiac surgery for another indication. During a **mean of 3.8 years** follow-up, ischaemic stroke or systemic embolism occurred in **114 patients (4.8%)** in the occlusion group and **168 (7.0%)** in the control arm (**HR, 0.67; 95% CI, 0.53–0.85; P = .001**).401"
  - **分数の代わりに実数＋% が原文にある**: 114 (4.8%) vs 168 (7.0%)。追跡 "mean of 3.8 years"（median (IQR) の記載なし）。
  - 重要な但し書き（原文逐語）:
    > "The LAAOS III trial **did not compare appendage occlusion with anticoagulation (77% of participants continued to receive OAC)**, and therefore, **surgical LAA closure should be considered as an adjunct therapy** to prevent thromboembolism **in addition to anticoagulation** in patients with AF."
- **endoscopic/hybrid ablation 時の LAA 閉鎖** 原文逐語:
  > "**There are no RCT data showing a beneficial effect on ischaemic stroke or systemic embolism in patients with AF undergoing LAAO during endoscopic or hybrid AF ablation.** A meta-analysis of RCT and observational data showed **no differences in stroke prevention or all-cause mortality** when comparing LAA clipping during thoracoscopic AF ablation with percutaneous LAAO and catheter ablation.402 While the percutaneous LAAO/catheter ablation group showed a **higher acute success rate**, it was also associated with a **higher risk of haemorrhage during the peri-operative period**."
  - 効果量・CI・P の記載は**一切なし**。402 = Zhang S et al. Front Cardiovasc Med 2022;9:970847。
- **クリップデバイス（AtriClip）の観察研究** 原文逐語:
  > "In an observational study evaluating **222 AF patients** undergoing LAA closure using a clipping device as a part of endoscopic or hybrid AF ablation, **complete closure was achieved in 95% of patients**.403 There were **no intra-operative complications**, and freedom from a combined endpoint of ischaemic stroke, haemorrhagic stroke, or TIA was **99.1% over 369 patient-years** of follow-up."
  - 95% / 99.1% とも **原文は % のみ**（分数の記載なし）、CI・P 値の記載なし。403 = van Laar C et al. JACC Clin Electrophysiol 2018;4:893–901。
- **AF 既往のない心臓手術患者への予防的 LAA 閉鎖** 原文逐語:
  > "Trials evaluating the beneficial effect of surgical LAA closure in patients undergoing cardiac surgery **but without a known history of AF are ongoing (NCT03724318, NCT02701062).404"
  （404 = Kiviniemi T et al. **LAA-CLOSURE** trial protocol, Am Heart J 2021;237:127–34）
- **stand-alone epicardial LAA closure** 原文逐語:
  > "There is a potential advantage for stand-alone epicardial over percutaneous LAA closure in patients with a contraindication for OAC, as **there is no need for post-procedure anticoagulation after epicardial closure**. Observational data show that stand-alone LAA closure using an epicardial clip is feasible and safe.405 A multidisciplinary team approach can facilitate the choice between epicardial or percutaneous LAA closure in such patients.406 **The majority of safety data and experience in epicardial LAA closure originate from a single clipping device (AtriClip)**403,407,408"

### 4.4 §9.6 POAF — 外科医に関わる数値

- 頻度（原文逐語）:
  > "Post-operative AF (POAF), defined as new-onset AF in the immediate post-operative period, is a common complication with clinical impact that occurs in **30%–50% of patients undergoing cardiac surgery**,816–818 and in **5%–30% of patients undergoing non-cardiac surgery**."
- 予後（原文逐語）:

## 7. 洞調律維持の意義（本レビューのテーマとの接点）

- 本ガイドラインは **症状改善が rhythm control の第一義**という立場を維持しつつ、選択された集団では予後改善を認める（原文逐語）:
  > "Based on all of these studies, this task force concludes that implementation of a rhythm control strategy can be safely instituted and confers amelioration of AF-related symptoms. **Beyond control of symptoms, sinus rhythm maintenance should also be pursued to reduce morbidity and mortality in selected groups of patients.**4,17,502,513,514"
- 根拠試験は **CASTLE-AF（ref 4）、EAST-AFNET 4（ref 17）、CASTLE-HTx（ref 513）**。EAST-AFNET 4 については（原文逐語）:
  > "EAST-AFNET 4 ... reported that implementation of a rhythm control strategy within 1 year compared with usual care significantly reduced the risk of cardiovascular death, stroke, or hospitalization for heart failure or acute coronary syndrome in patients older than 75 years or with cardiovascular conditions.17 Of note, **rhythm control was predominantly pursued with antiarrhythmic drugs (80% of patients in the intervention arm)**. ... Patients in the EAST-AFNET 4 trial all had cardiovascular risk factors but were at an early stage of AF, with **more than 50% being in sinus rhythm and 30% being asymptomatic** at the start of the study."
  - 効果量・95%CI・P 値の記載は本文に**なし**。
- CABANA は否定的（原文逐語）:
  > "the CABANA trial ... **could not demonstrate a significant difference in mortality and morbidity** between catheter ablation and standard rhythm and/or rate control drugs in symptomatic AF patients older than 64 years, or younger than 65 years with risk factors for stroke.3"
- **重要**: 外科的アブレーションの推奨文（22-1, 22-3）の目的語は一貫して **"to prevent symptoms and recurrence of AF"** であり、**卒中・死亡の予防は目的として掲げられていない**。§7.2.8 本文も "further RCTs are needed to evaluate ... whether this approach contributes to the prevention of stroke, thromboembolism, and death." と明記している。

## 8. 限界

### 【著者（タスクフォース）記載】
- §7.2.8: "**further RCTs are needed to evaluate which patients should be selected, and whether this approach contributes to the prevention of stroke, thromboembolism, and death**."
- §7.2.7: hybrid RCT は "**typically performed in highly experienced centres**"（一般化可能性の限界）。
- §7.2.7: "endoscopic and hybrid ablation have **higher complication rates than catheter ablation**"。
- §6.6: "**There are no RCT data** showing a beneficial effect on ischaemic stroke or systemic embolism in patients with AF undergoing LAAO during endoscopic or hybrid AF ablation."
- §6.6: LAAOS III は抗凝固との比較ではない（77% が OAC 継続）ため、外科的 LAA 閉鎖は **adjunct** に留まる。
- §12 Gaps in evidence の [R] 項（外科に関連しうる記載、原文逐語）:
  > "The amount of AF reduction obtained by rhythm control to improve outcomes is unknown."
  > "Large catheter ablation studies showed no improved outcome of patients with AF. Some small studies in specific subpopulations have observed an improved outcome. This warrants further investigation to provide each patient with AF with personalized treatment goals."
  > "Large variability in ablation strategies and techniques exist for patients with persistent AF, or after first failed catheter ablation for paroxysmal AF. The optimal catheter ablation strategy and techniques, however, are unknown."
  > "**Sham-controlled intervention studies are lacking** to determine the effects on AF symptoms, quality of life, and PROMS, accounting for the placebo effect that is associated with interventions."
  → **Gaps in evidence のリストに「surgical ablation」を名指しした項目は存在しない**（外科は [R] 全体の記載に含意されるのみ）。

### 【読み手として】
1. **本文に外科的アブレーションの有効性の定量値が一切ない。** §7.2.8 で数値が示されるのはペースメーカ率（6.0% vs 4.1%, RR 1.69, 95%CI 1.12–2.54）のみ。freedom from AF、卒中、死亡の効果量は本文に無く、supplementary Evidence Table 22 / Additional Evidence Table S25 に外出しされている。**統合レビューでこの推奨の数値的裏づけを書くには、根拠文献（683–685, 687, 701）を一次資料として別途精読する必要がある。**
2. **Class I A（僧帽弁手術）の "A" は RCT メタ解析（683–685）に依拠しているが、それらのメタ解析のアウトカムは主に「洞調律維持」であり、Class I の "beneficial, useful, effective" が指すのは症状・再発の抑制であって生命予後ではない。** 推奨文の目的節が "to prevent symptoms and recurrence of AF" に限定されている点を読み違えないこと。
3. **僧帽弁手術と非僧帽弁手術で Class を分けた（I A vs IIa B）のが本版の最大の実務的変更。** 2020 年版は「心臓手術全般で IIa A」だったため、CABG/AVR 単独への同時アブレーションは**形式上は格下げ**（IIa A → IIa B）に相当する。この点は本文に明示されていない（**読み手の推論**）。
4. **エネルギー源・lesion set・biatrial vs left-sided の指針が皆無。** "bipolar radiofrequency and/or cryothermy" と "transmural lesions including PVI" 以外の技術的記述はなく、STS 2017 ガイドライン（ref 681）に委ねられている。
5. **リズム判定法（モニタリング手段）の規定が外科アブレーションに関して存在しない。** §7.2.5 のカテーテルアブレーション文脈で "Intermittent rhythm monitoring has typically been used to detect AF relapses following catheter ablation" と述べ、smartwatch/PPG/wearable patch/ILR に言及するのみ。**blanking period の定義、>30 秒の再発閾値、AT/AFL を失敗に含めるか、AAD の扱いについての規定は本ガイドライン本文に一切ない**（§3 の clinical AF 定義で "Periods of 30 s or more may indicate clinical concern" とあるが、これはアブレーション後再発の定義ではない）。
6. **AF 再発リスク因子（左房径、AF 罹病期間など）の記述が 2024 年版の推奨文から消えた。** 2020 年版の推奨文には "(left atrial dilatation, years in AF, age, renal dysfunction, and other cardiovascular risk factors)" が明示されていたが、2024 年版は "AF suitable for a rhythm control strategy" という抽象表現に置き換わり、選択基準の操作的定義が失われている。
7. **PMI 率の 2 つの数値（6.0%/4.1% と 2.1%）は異なる集団・異なるデザインの数値で、直接比較できない。** 前者は 22 RCT の Cochrane、後者は 2011–2020 のレジストリ。

## 9. 本レビューにとっての含意（外科への含意）

原著（ガイドライン）の主張:
1. **僧帽弁手術＋AF → 同時外科的アブレーションは Class I, Level A。** 2020 年版の Class IIa A から格上げされた。ただし目的は "to prevent symptoms and recurrence of AF"。
2. **非僧帽弁（CABG・AVR 等）＋AF → Class IIa, Level B。** 根拠は Cochrane 701 と観察研究 703–707 が中心。
3. **どちらも "with shared decision-making supported by an experienced team of electrophysiologists and arrhythmia surgeons" が推奨文本体に組み込まれている。** ハートチーム要件が推奨文の構成要素。
4. **外科アブレーション時の術中血栓検索イメージング（例 TOE）は Class I, Level C（新規推奨）。** 抗凝固が効いていても実施。
5. **外科的 LAA 閉鎖は心臓手術時 Class I, Level B（LAAOS III）。ただし抗凝固の "adjunct" であり、閉鎖したから OAC を止めるという運用は明確に否定されている（Rec 21-1, Class I C）。**
6. **持続性 AF に対する endoscopic/hybrid ablation は IIb C → IIa A へ大幅格上げ。** 一方、発作性 AF に対しては IIa B → IIb B へ格下げされ、**「カテーテルアブレーション失敗後」に限定**された。すなわち胸腔鏡/ハイブリッドの適応は「発作性の一次治療」から「持続性の一次〜二次治療」へ重心が移動した。
7. **posterior pericardiotomy が POAF 予防として Class IIa B で新規収載**（OR 0.44; 95%CI 0.27–0.70; P = .0005）。心臓外科医が術中に実行できる唯一の POAF 予防手技として推奨された。
8. **POAF に対する長期 OAC は IIa B へ格上げ**（心臓手術・非心臓手術いずれも）。

読み手（統合レビュー執筆者）としての推論:
9. **「Maze IV / Cox-maze」という語は Recommendation には一切登場せず、本文で "The best validated method of surgical ablation is the Maze procedure" と述べられるのみ。** ESC/EACTS は術式名を推奨に書き込まず、"concomitant surgical ablation" という包括語を採用している。lesion set の具体は STS 2017 ガイドライン（ref 681）を参照するしかない。
10. **PFA（pulsed field ablation）は本ガイドラインでカテーテルアブレーションの文脈で 1 箇所のみ言及**（"In a single-blind RCT of 607 patients, pulsed field ablation was non-inferior for efficacy and safety endpoints compared with conventional radiofrequency or cryoballoon ablation.601"）。**外科的 PFA / epicardial PFA への言及は皆無**であり、2024 年時点のガイドラインは外科 PFA を扱っていない。
11. **本ガイドラインは、外科的アブレーションを「症状・再発の治療」と位置づけ、「予後改善のための治療」とは位置づけていない。** 統合レビューで生命予後改善を論じる場合、本ガイドラインを根拠にしてはならず、観察研究（689, 691, 694, 699 等）を一次資料として扱う必要がある。
