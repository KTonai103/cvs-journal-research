## 1. 文書の位置づけ

- 2017年 STS CPG（Badhwar V, Rankin JS, Damiano RJ, et al. Ann Thorac Surg. 2017;103:329-341）の**5年更新版**。
- 更新の駆動要因は原文で次のとおり: "Substantial new evidence regarding the risks and benefits of surgical left atrial appendage occlusion and the long-term benefits of surgical ablation has been produced in the last 5 years."
- Abstract に明示された3つの主変更点（逐語）:
  > "Compared with the 2017 clinical practice guideline, the current update has an emphasis on surgical ablation in first-time, nonemergent cardiac surgery and its long-term benefits, an extension of the recommendation to perform surgical ablation in all patients with atrial fibrillation undergoing first-time, nonemergent cardiac surgery, and a new class I recommendation for left atrial appendage occlusion in all patients with atrial fibrillation undergoing first-time, nonemergent cardiac surgery."
- さらに新設された領域:
  > "Further guidance is provided for patients with structural heart disease and atrial fibrillation being considered for transcatheter valve repair or replacement, as well as patients in need of isolated left atrial appendage management who are not candidates for surgical ablation."
- 対象は3つの手術カテゴリーに区分（2017年版を踏襲）: "primary open atrial operations, primary closed atrial operations, and stand-alone operations for atrial fibrillation."
  - open atrial = mitral valve repair or replacement
  - closed atrial = AVR、CABG、AVR+CABG
  - stand-alone = 単独SAまたは単独LAA閉鎖

## 2. 方法論（エビデンス評価法・COI）

- 作成主体: **STS Workforce on Evidence-Based Surgery** が2022年に writing group を招集。
- 文献検索: **MEDLINE と Embase**。制限は "human subjects in English between January 1, 2017, and January 1, 2023"、2017年版と同一の検索アルゴリズム。手作業での追加あり。
  - **"More than 1143 results were obtained."**（原文はこの表現。ヒット件数の正確な内訳は記載なし）
  - 除外基準: case reports／AFの発生率・リスク因子のみを扱う population-based study／非外科手技が主眼のもの／本ガイドラインの焦点外のアウトカム・マーカーを扱うもの。
  - **"A total of 35 relevant new articles met the inclusion criteria and were analyzed in detail by the writing group."**（＝新規精読論文は35編のみ。残りは既存2017年版のエビデンスベース）
- バイアス評価: 観察研究は **Newcastle-Ottawa scale**（Supplemental Table 2）。RCT・メタ解析は **Center on Evidence Based Medicine** 推奨に準じたチェックリスト。全データを evidence tables に集約。
- **task force 自身によるメタ解析を実施**: "Select meta-analyses were conducted where data from multiple studies were deemed adequate and reasonably similar to justify the pooling of estimates using random effect models (long-term outcomes with surgical ablation; concomitant left atrial appendage occlusion)."
  - ただし "Only published meta-analyses were included in the main guideline document. Meta-analyses conducted by the task force are in the Supplemental Materials (Supplemental Figures A-H)."
  - → **本文中の Figure 2 と Supplemental Figures D-H の pooled estimate は「本ガイドライン自身が新規に行ったランダム効果メタ解析」**である点は本レビューで重要。
- 合意形成: "A modified Delphi consensus process and anonymous electronic voting was used to approve the final recommendations (Supplemental Table 3)."（Executive Summary では "After a review of all new data and confidential voting, the consensus of the writing group was..."）
- グレーディング体系: **"The recommendations are graded according to the American College of Cardiology/American Heart Association Recommendation System."**（ACC/AHA方式。ゆえに COR = I / IIa / IIb / III、LOE = A / B-NR / C の表記）
- 承認経路: STS Workforce on Evidence-Based Surgery → STS Council on Quality and Research → STS Executive Committee。
- 準拠規範: **Institute of Medicine standards**（5年ごとの更新規定を含む）。

## 3. 外科的AFアブレーション・LAA管理に関する推奨（Table 1 全9項目・逐語）

★**表の整合性確認**: pdftotext の Table 1 では COR/LOE の列が本文列から分離して出力されている。COR/LOE のペアは上から順に 9 組（I/A, I/B-NR, IIa/B-NR, IIa/B-NR, III/C, I/A, IIb/B-NR, IIa/B-NR, I/C）、推奨文も 9 本で **数は 1 対 1 に一致**。さらに Table 2（新規・変更推奨の対比表）および本文各節末尾の "• Class of recommendation / • Level of evidence" 箇条書きにより、**9項目すべてが独立に二重確認できた**（下記に各推奨の本文中該当行を併記）。行ズレなしと判断。

### 3-1. Recommendations for mitral valve operations
> "Surgical ablation for atrial fibrillation is recommended for first-time nonemergent concomitant mitral operations to restore sinus rhythm and improve long-term outcomes."
- **COR: I ／ LOE: A**
- 本文 Section 1（SURGICAL ABLATION AND CONCOMITANT MITRAL VALVE OPERATIONS）末尾で "• Class of recommendation: I" "• Level of evidence: A" と再掲。

### 3-2. Recommendations for operations other than mitral valve surgery
> "Surgical ablation for atrial fibrillation is recommended for any first-time nonemergent concomitant nonmitral operation to restore sinus rhythm and improve long-term outcomes."
- **COR: I ／ LOE: B-NR**
- 本文 Section 2（SURGICAL ABLATION DURING CONCOMITANT CLOSED-ATRIAL NONMITRAL VALVE SURGERY）末尾で再掲。
- ★**2017年版が "isolated aortic valve replacement, isolated coronary artery bypass graft surgery, and aortic valve replacement plus coronary artery bypass graft operations" と術式を列挙していたのに対し、2023年版は "any first-time nonemergent concomitant nonmitral operation" と全非僧帽弁手術へ拡大**（COR/LOEはI/B-NRのまま不変、文言のみ強化）。

### 3-3. Recommendations regarding stand-alone surgical ablation（3項目）
> (1) "Surgical ablation for symptomatic atrial fibrillation in the absence of structural heart disease refractory to class I/III antiarrhythmic drugs, catheter-based therapy, or both, is reasonable as a primary stand-alone procedure to restore sinus rhythm."
- **COR: IIa ／ LOE: B-NR**

> (2) "Surgical ablation for symptomatic persistent or longstanding persistent atrial fibrillation in the absence of structural heart disease is reasonable as a stand-alone procedure using the Cox maze III/IV lesion set as the preferred procedure."
- **COR: IIa ／ LOE: B-NR**

> (3) "Surgical ablation for symptomatic atrial fibrillation in the setting of left atrial enlargement (≥4.5 cm) or more than moderate mitral regurgitation by pulmonary vein isolation alone is not recommended."
- **COR: III ／ LOE: C**
- ★この3項目は Table 2（新規・変更推奨表）に**掲載されていない** → 2017年版から COR/LOE ともに**不変**（Abstract: "along with the other recommendations from the 2017 guidelines that remained unchanged in their class of recommendation and level of evidence"）。

### 3-4. Recommendations for concomitant left atrial appendage management
> "Left atrial appendage obliteration for atrial fibrillation is recommended for all first-time nonemergent cardiac surgery procedures, with or without concomitant surgical ablation, to reduce morbidity from thromboembolic complications."
- **COR: I ／ LOE: A**
- ★**本改訂の最大の変更点**。2017年版は2項目（"It is reasonable to perform left atrial appendage excision or exclusion in conjunction with surgical ablation for atrial fibrillation for longitudinal thromboembolic morbidity prevention." = IIa/C／"At the time of concomitant cardiac operations in patients with atrial fibrillation, it is reasonable to surgically manage the left atrial appendage for longitudinal thromboembolic morbidity prevention." = IIa/C）だったのが、**IIa/C → I/A へ2段階格上げ**、かつ**SA併施の有無を問わず全例**に拡大。

### 3-5. Recommendations regarding stand-alone left atrial appendage management（新設）
> "Isolated surgical left atrial appendage obliteration may be considered in patients with longstanding persistent atrial fibrillation, a high stroke risk, and contraindications for or failure of long-term oral anticoagulation."
- **COR: IIb ／ LOE: B-NR**
- ★2017年版に対応項目なし＝**完全新設**（Table 2 の2017年欄が空白）。

### 3-6. Recommendations for patients being considered for transcatheter valve therapies（新設）
> "For patients with symptomatic valve disease and atrial fibrillation, who are deemed of low to intermediate surgical risk, surgical valve repair or replacement with concomitant surgical ablation and left atrial appendage occlusion is reasonable over isolated transcatheter valve repair or replacement alone to restore sinus rhythm and improve long-term outcomes."
- **COR: IIa ／ LOE: B-NR**
- ★**完全新設**。TAVR/TEER全盛時代に対する外科側からの明示的カウンター推奨。

### 3-7. Recommendations for all patients with atrial fibrillation（新設）
> "Multidisciplinary heart team assessment and treatment planning as well as long-term follow-up using periodic continuous electrocardiographic monitoring for rhythm assessment, are recommended to optimize patient outcomes."
- **COR: I ／ LOE: C**
- ★Table 2 には掲載されていないが、Abstract は "The importance of a multidisciplinary team assessment, treatment planning, and long-term follow-up are reiterated in this clinical practice guideline with a class I recommendation" と述べる。Table 1 と本文 Section 7 末尾で I/C。
- 注: Table 1 の文言には推奨文中に "monitoring for rhythm assessment, are recommended" とコンマがあるが、本文 Section 7 の再掲版はコンマなし（"...for rhythm assessment are recommended..."）。実質同文。

## 4. リズム判定・モニタリングに関する本文の記述 ★

ガイドラインは**リズム判定法の不統一を明示的に問題視**している。逐語で:

- 成功の定義（本文 Section 7）:
  > "After surgical ablation, it is suggested that patients be longitudinally monitored for a minimum of 1 year by the surgeon of the multidisciplinary heart team. **The measure of success in surgical ablation is freedom from atrial fibrillation and antiarrhythmic drugs at 1 year.**"
- サーベイランス期間:
  > "To detect the late recurrence of atrial fibrillation and the development of new conduction abnormalities, continued surveillance for up to 5 years is suggested."
- モニタリング手段:
  > "A good follow-up is essential, and at least periodic 24-hour Holter monitoring should be routine."
  - ★**「最低限 periodic 24-hour Holter」**が本文の記述。一方 Table 1 / Section 7 の推奨文では **"periodic continuous electrocardiographic monitoring"** と表現される（COR I / LOE C）。**具体的な頻度・デバイス（7日イベント／ILR／連続モニタ）の指定は原文になし**。
- 既存文献の質に対する批判（stand-alone の項）:
  > "Many of these reports do not always adhere to continuous electrocardiographic monitoring to support the primary rhythm end point."
  > "These were not always documented by continuous electrocardiographic monitoring to support the rhythm end points."
- エビデンス欠落の自認:
  > "There is no literature specifically addressing the clinical questions surrounding surveillance and follow-up after surgical ablation."

**blanking period・>30秒閾値・AT/AFL を失敗に含めるか**については、本ガイドライン原文に**定義の記載なし**。AAD の扱いのみ「1年時点で AF free かつ AAD free」を成功と定義する旨が記載されている。

## 5. 各推奨の根拠として引用されている試験・データセット（原文にある数値のみ）

### 5-1. 同時併施SA（僧帽弁）
- **CTSN RCT（Gillinov AM, et al. ref 46）**: 逐語 —
  > "In the Cardiothoracic Surgical Trials Network randomized clinical trial, 260 patients with persistent or long-standing persistent atrial fibrillation underwent pulmonary vein isolation, a biatrial maze procedure, or no ablation during concomitant mitral valve surgery. Ablation resulted in significant higher freedom from atrial fibrillation at 12 months (63% vs 29%), a higher risk of permanent pacemaker implantation, and lower mortality (6.8% vs 8.7%), although this did not reach statistical significance."
  - 63% vs 29%、6.8% vs 8.7% とも **95%CI・P値の記載なし（原文は%のみ）**。分数の記載なし。
- **ポーランド全国レジストリ（ref 71）**: "a national registry study from the Republic of Poland including 3568 patients undergoing mitral valve surgery with a median follow-up of 5 years showed reduced all-cause mortality with surgical ablation (**hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% CI, 0.7-0.96**)."（P値の記載なし）
- **Kim ら リウマチ性MV（ref 69）**: "monitoring 1229 patients for more than 5 years"。効果量の数値記載なし。
- **RCT16件のメタ解析（ref 44）**: 主に僧帽弁だが他術式も含む。isolated AVR と CABG の両サブグループで1年時点の洞調律有病率がSA群で高い。"There were no significant differences between the ablation and no-ablation groups regarding all-cause mortality, pacemaker implantation, and neurologic events."（効果量・CI・P値の記載なし）
- **ネットワークメタ解析 2031例（ref 100）**: "suggested greater effectiveness with more extensive ablation, including open left atrial or biatrial maze vs PVI alone. However, this study showed no further improvement in conversion to sinus rhythm with a biatrial maze. The left atrial maze was also not associated with increased operative morbidity or mortality compared with PVI alone. The network meta-analysis did suggest increased mortality with the addition of the right atrial maze; importantly, the individuals included in the biatrial maze group underwent more complex procedures and were higher-risk patients."（効果量の数値記載なし）
- **腎障害（ref 79・単施設）**: AKI の "odds ratio, 1.89; 95% CI, 1.12-3.18; P = .017"、CPB時間とは独立。ただし "there was not an increased risk of permanent dialysis, nor did the higher incidence of acute kidney injury impact the survival benefit seen with surgical ablation."
- **ペースメーカー（STSデータベース ref 78）**: CABG／弁手術／その併施での同時SA施行例で PPM 必要性が高い —
  > "(7.8% vs 5.9%; risk-adjusted odds ratio, 1.33; 95% CI, 1.24-1.43; P < .0001)"
  - 分母（分数）の記載なし。原文は%とOR/CI/Pのみ。
- **単施設観察（ref 60）**: "a 2-fold to 3-fold higher incidence of pacemaker implantation among patients undergoing ablation vs patients undergoing mitral valve surgery alone."（CI・P値の記載なし）
- **早期PPM必要率の幅**: "has spanned a wide range from 1.6% (ref 80) to 14% (ref 81)."
- 変換不良と関連する因子（逐語）:
  > "Variables that are consistently found to be associated with lower rates of conversion to sinus rhythm after surgical ablation include large left atrium, advanced age, cumulative atrial fibrillation burden, a less extensive ablation procedure, in particular, omission or incompleteness of the left posterior wall isolation, and the coronary sinus lesion."
  - ★lesion set 論として本レビューで最も引用価値の高い一文。**LA後壁隔離の省略／不完全化と冠静脈洞ライン欠如を失敗因子として名指し**している。
- 早期POAFの減少: "Several randomized clinical trials of mitral patients have established surgical ablation as an effective intervention to reduce the prevalence of early postoperative atrial fibrillation by more than 50%."（refs 40-43、効果量の数値記載なし）

### 5-2. 同時併施SA（非僧帽弁・closed atrial）
- **Cox maze III + CABG 早期研究（Damiano RJ, et al. 2003, ref 90）**: "produced a 98% sinus rhythm rate at 5 years."（CI/P記載なし）
- **CABG±PVI の小規模RCT 35例（ref 94）**: "At 18 months, 89% of patients in the PVI group were atrial fibrillation-free vs 47% in the coronary artery bypass grafting-only group."（原文は%のみ、分数・CI・P値の記載なし）
- **lesion set による成功率の差（重要）**:
  > "in these specific patient cohorts, rhythm end point recovery seems to approximate **50% to 80% with PVI alone** compared with **>90% with full open atrial biatrial maze procedures**."（refs 90, 98, 99）
- **CABG + Cox maze III/IV vs CABG + PVI vs CABG alone の2014年RCT（ref 95）**: "reported no in-hospital mortality or difference in major morbidity."
- **台湾全国レジストリ（ref 67）**: "including nearly 7000 patients undergoing coronary artery bypass grafting, valve, and aortic procedures, demonstrated a significant reduction in all-cause mortality, stroke, and thromboembolism over 5 years."（効果量・CI・P値の記載なし）
- **Medicare 100,000例（Mehaffey JH, et al. JTCVS 2024, ref 101）**: "surgical ablation was associated with reduced 3-year mortality and a significant reduction in stroke with surgical ablation when compared with **left atrial appendage occlusion alone**."（効果量・CI・P値の記載なし。★比較対照が「LAAO単独」である点が重要）
- **★本ガイドライン自身のプール推定（Figure 2 / Supplemental Figures E, F）** — 2年超の長期フォロー研究をランダム効果モデルでプール:
  > "the advantage of surgical ablation over no ablation past the 2-year mark was as follows: all-cause mortality (hazard ratio [HR], **0.77; 95% CI, 0.69-0.86**) (Figure 2), stroke (HR, **0.69; 95% CI, 0.64-0.75**), and thromboembolism (HR, **0.68; 95% CI, 0.63-0.74**)."
  - Figure 2 の凡例（逐語、文字間隔崩れを補正）: "Studies with a follow-up of ≥2 years (median follow-up range, 2.6-6.7 years; n = 8 studies) and the pooled effect estimate for all-cause mortality associated with surgical ablation of atrial fibrillation is shown (estimate [ES], 0.77; 95% CI, 0.69-0.86)."
  - **P値の記載なし。異質性（I²）の記載なし。**
- **★PPMのコスト（Supplemental Figure H）**: "Pooling the effect estimates from 3 studies with at least 2 years of follow-up and reported permanent pacemaker implantation information suggests that the long-term mortality benefit of surgical ablation is at the cost of an increased chance of requiring a permanent pacemaker (**HR, 1.38; 95% CI, 1.22-1.57**)."（P値の記載なし）
- 安全性の総括: "The addition of surgical ablation to any concomitant, nonemergent nonmitral cardiac surgery procedure can be considered safe based on currently available evidence."（refs 78, 102）／"Of the large number of studies that have investigated the difference between surgical ablation and no surgical ablation in a heterogenous surgical population, none has demonstrated an obvious association of surgical ablation with short-term major morbidity or mortality."

### 5-3. Stand-alone SA・胸腔鏡下・ハイブリッド
- 適応の原則: "The presence of symptomatic atrial fibrillation refractory to at least 1 class I or III antiarrhythmic drug has been established in several prior societal guidelines as the primary indication for ablation in stand-alone patients. Often, most patients also have had at least 1 unsuccessful catheter-based ablation before referral for stand-alone surgical ablation."
- **Cox maze IV ≒ Cox maze III**: "One systematic review suggested the efficacy of bipolar radiofrequency plus cryoablation (Cox maze IV) to complete the lesion set was equivalent to the Cox maze III technique for stand-alone surgical ablation, **if both were applied meticulously**."（refs 97, 106）
- 胸腔鏡下 off-pump RF PVI + LAA切除: "For the 60% to 80% of patients who attain the rhythm end point, antiarrhythmic and anticoagulant agents are eventually discontinued, with associated improved quality of life."（refs 107-113）／"In most series, paroxysmal cases had a higher conversion rate than persistent atrial fibrillation."
- **外科 vs カテーテル（一般論）**: "surgical approaches in most of the studies was found to be more successful in restoring and maintaining sinus rhythm than catheter-based ablation, but at the cost of higher periprocedural morbidity."（refs 24, 115-120）
- **例外となる小規模RCT（ref 121, n=52, paroxysmal AF）**: "transcatheter PVI or thoracoscopic surgical PVI isolation plus left atrial appendage occlusion, showing **56% freedom of atrial fibrillation at 2 years in the transcatheter arm vs 29% freedom in the surgical arm**, although this difference did not reach statistical significance and the findings are relatively fragile, given the small sample size."（CI・P値の記載なし。★外科側に不利な唯一のRCTとして明記されている）
- **PVI単独の限界**: "Isolated PVI has not performed as well as minimally invasive versions of the full on-pump endocardial maze procedure."（refs 122-124）
- **ganglionated plexi（GP）アブレーションの否定**: "ganglionic ablation has been minimally efficacious and is no longer considered as an additive application to surgical ablation. In a randomized clinical trial, additional ganglionic ablation did not result in greater procedural success but significantly increased major morbidity, including bleeding, sinus node dysfunction, and need for permanent pacemaker implantation."（refs 58, 125, 126。効果量の記載なし）
- **lesion set の原則**: "Most studies suggest the relative superiority of extended left atrial and biatrial lesion sets over PVI, with more extensive ablation patterns producing the best atrial fibrillation conversion rates. **Surgeons should be aware that incomplete lesion sets can be proarrhythmic and have been implicated in the induction of atypical macro-reentrant atrial flutter.**"（refs 96, 127, 128）
- **ハイブリッドアブレーション**（本レビューの主要論点）:
  - 定義: "the percutaneous endocardial technique is combined with a minimally invasive epicardial nonsternotomy ablation that does not include cardiopulmonary bypass."（ref 132）
  - 系統的レビュー（ref 133 として引用されているが、参考文献133は Van Praet KM らの minimally invasive SA + rheumatic の4年追跡研究であり、**引用番号と参照内容の対応にズレの疑いあり**。数値は本文に無し）: "revealed that at 12 months or more, the hybrid procedure achieved a significantly higher rate of freedom from atrial arrhythmias, with and without the use of antiarrhythmic drugs, compared with atrial fibrillation catheter ablation alone in patients with persistent or long-standing persistent atrial fibrillation."
  - **CEASE-AF RCT（ref 25 = Doll N, et al. EClinicalMedicine 2023;61:102052）**: "comparing hybrid ablation against catheter ablation alone in patients with persistent atrial fibrillation showed significantly higher effectiveness with hybrid ablation (**71% vs 39%**), with no significant difference in major complications at 30 days."（原文は%のみ。分数・CI・P値の記載なし）
  - **HARTCAP-AF（ref 135 = van der Heijden CAJ, et al. JACC Clin Electrophysiol 2023;9:1013-1023）と CONVERGE（ref 24 = DeLurgio DB, et al. Circ Arrhythm Electrophysiol 2020;13:e009288）**:
    > "both randomized clinical trials in patients with persistent atrial fibrillation, demonstrated the superiority of the hybrid ablation strategy (**89% and 65% success at 12 months**) over catheter ablation (**41% and 37%**), with comparable adverse events in both groups."
    - ★本文の引用番号は "24,134" と付されているが、ref 134 は Khan Z らの review（J Card Surg 2020）であり **HARTCAP-AF の一次文献ではない**。**89%/41% が HARTCAP-AF、65%/37% が CONVERGE に対応すると読むのが文脈上自然だが、原文で明示的な対応づけはされていない（列挙順に一致すると推定されるのみ）**。CI・P値の記載なし。統合レビューで数値を使う場合は一次文献で確認すべき。
  - メタ解析による裏づけ: refs 135-137（Eranki A, et al. J Cardiothorac Surg 2023;18:155／Baudo M, et al. Int J Cardiol 2023;376:62-75 ほか）。効果量の記載なし。
- **総括的な立場（重要・逐語）**:
  > "Considering the totality of the evidence, more complete lesion sets, particularly a biatrial Cox maze III/IV procedure, applied through a minimally invasive approach, may provide the greatest benefit overall."
  > "An increasing number of stand-alone surgical ablation studies using cryoablation and a minimally invasive thoracoscopic or robotic-assisted approach have recently shown improved outcomes in safety and effectiveness, with **>90% of patients being free from atrial fibrillation at 1 year and 80% at 5 years of follow up**. Notably, these results were achieved at established, high-volume centers despite including many patients with long-standing persistent atrial fibrillation. These were not always documented by continuous electrocardiographic monitoring to support the rhythm end points."（refs 26, 138-142。CI・P値の記載なし）
  > "Given stronger longitudinal evidence of efficacy and longitudinal freedom from atrial fibrillation, antiarrhythmic drugs, as well as oral anticoagulation after a full biatrial Cox maze, the field awaits more homogeneous or randomized evidence on hybrid or epicardial ablation procedures that adhere to the concept of the Cox maze lesion set. **Epicardial ablation with atypical lesions remains exploratory until more robust evidence becomes available.**"
  - ★★本レビューにとって決定的な一文。**ハイブリッド／心外膜アブレーションは有望だが、Cox maze lesion set の概念から逸脱した「atypical lesions」は "exploratory" とされ、推奨文には昇格していない**（stand-alone の推奨は依然 "Cox maze III/IV lesion set as the preferred procedure" = IIa/B-NR）。
- 左房縮小術: "Because left atrial size is a risk factor for ablation failure, atrial reduction procedures may have benefit, although this remains controversial and without strong evidence."（refs 141-144）
- **Stand-alone の安全性データ**:
  - Krul ら 2013 系統的レビュー（ref 145、23観察研究・752例、低侵襲stand-alone）: "operative mortality was 0.4%. Complication rates attributed to surgery were just 3.2%."

### 5-4. 同時併施LAA閉鎖（COR I / LOE A の根拠）

  - ★★**"95% CI, 0.79-0.55" は下限>上限であり、原著誌面の誤植または pdftotext の読み取り誤りの疑いが濃厚**。原文表記のまま記載したが、**この CI は統合レビューで採用しないこと**を推奨。P値の記載なし。

- 小規模施設研究: "Several smaller institutional studies of patients undergoing valve procedures, coronary artery bypass grafting, and combinations thereof were unable to demonstrate the same effect convincingly, possibly due to a much smaller sample size and shorter follow up."（refs 88, 149）

- **メタ解析6件（refs 152-156）**: 最大規模（**n = 40,107**、ref 155 = Mohamed MMG, et al. Am J Cardiol 2021）—
  > "showed a substantially lower incidence of stroke (**RR, 0.68; 95% CI, 0.57-0.82**) and thromboembolic complications (**RR, 0.63; 95% CI, 0.53-0.76**)."
  - "Qualitatively and quantitatively, very similar results for all-cause mortality, stroke, and thromboembolic complications were found by the 5 other meta-analyses across different time periods, follow-up durations, and case-mix, even including left ventricular assist device populations."（他5件の個別数値は記載なし）
- **閉鎖法の優劣（★lesion/技術論として重要）**:
  > "In these observational studies, management of the left atrial appendage by resection, epicardial stapling, clip application, or endoatrial double-layer longitudinal suture closure has been studied extensively. **Stapling only has had poor outcomes, with most patients having a residual stump and recanalization of the appendage, which can be thrombogenic.**"（refs 157, 158）
  > "Complications from surgical left atrial appendage occlusion are rare but most frequently are related to the manipulation of the appendage, causing bleeding, and incorrect placement of the epicardial clip, leaving a residual stump or potentially impinging the circumflex coronary artery."（ref 159）
- **LAACS RCT（ref 161 = Madsen CV, et al. - **★LAAOS III（ref 160 = Whitlock RP, et al. N Engl J Med. 2021;384:*ページ番号は本テキストで欠落*）** — I/A への格上げの中核根拠。逐語:
  > "The larger Left Atrial Appendage Occlusion Study III (LAAOS III) randomized clinical trial enrolled **4770 patients** with atrial fibrillation undergoing concomitant cardiac surgery procedures to receive left atrial appendage occlusion or not with routine use of oral anticoagulation postoperatively and a **mean follow-up of 3.8 years**. Permissible methods of left atrial appendage occlusion in this trial included **amputation and closure (preferred), stapler closure, double-layer linear closure from within the atrium in participants undergoing minithoracotomy (this approach required transesophageal echocardiographic confirmation of the occlusion), or closure with an epicardial clip**. **Approximately one-third of patients also underwent a surgical ablation procedure, 92% of the individuals received the allocated left atrial appendage management, and 77% were compliant with oral anticoagulation at 3 years.** Despite ongoing oral anticoagulation in most of the patients, left atrial appendage occlusion significantly reduced the primary end point (ischemic stroke or systemic thromboembolism), primarily driven by a reduction in stroke (**RR, 0.66; 95% CI, 0.52-0.84**). However, in LAAOS III, the benefits of left atrial appendage occlusion **did not include a decrease in all-cause mortality**."
  - P値の記載なし。primary end point 自体の効果量（HR）は本ガイドライン本文に**記載なし**（記載されているのは stroke の RR 0.66 のみ）。
  - 二次解析（ref 162 = Connolly SJ, et al. Circulation 2023;148:1298-1304）: "found the reduction in thromboembolic complications with left atrial appendage occlusion was independent of the use of oral anticoagulation."（効果量の記載なし）

### 5-5. Stand-alone LAA閉鎖（COR IIb / LOE B-NR）
- クリップ: "Left atrial appendage occlusion using a clip has been found to be safe, effective with a **95% success rate of complete left atrial appendage occlusion based on cardiac computed tomographic imaging**, and reproducible in most hands."（refs 164-166）

### 5-6. TAVR/TEER 検討中患者（COR IIa / LOE B-NR、新設）
- **AFMR（atrial functional MR）への明示的言及（★本レビューの重要論点）** — 逐語:
  > "several valvular pathologies are known to progress with the persistence of atrial fibrillation, notably mitral and tricuspid regurgitation. A specific entity of atrioventricular valve insufficiency has been established in patients with persistent atrial fibrillation, resulting in a **type I dilated annulus and valve dysfunction (atrial functional mitral/tricuspid regurgitation)**. Nonetheless, transcatheter edge-to-edge repair has been promoted as a therapy for this subset of patients.**"（ref 175 = Farhan S, et al. JACC state-of-the-art review 2022;80:2314-2330）
  - ★AFMR に対して TEER 単独は "palliative"、SA+LAAO を伴う外科治療が "curative" という立場を明確に打ち出している。ただし**AFMR に対する具体的な数値エビデンスは本文に提示されていない**。
- TAVRランドマーク試験でのAF有病率: "In the transcatheter aortic valve replacement landmark trials, between one-third and one-half of the patients presented with atrial fibrillation at enrollment." / 低リスク試験2件では "15% to 18% of patients had atrial fibrillation at baseline, most of whom in either arm did not receive treatment."（refs 176, 177）
- 背景疫学: "From 30% to 50% of patients undergoing surgical or transcatheter aortic valve replacement present with atrial fibrillation."（refs 4-8）
- **Medicare大規模研究（ref 89 = Patrick WL, et al. Cardiol Ther 2022;11:283-296）** — AS+AF で SAVR+SA vs TAVR単独:
  > "resulting in reduced all-cause mortality (**HR, 0.65; 95% CI, 0.53-0.79**), permanent pacemaker implantation (**HR, 0.62; 95% CI, 0.44-0.87**), bleeding (**HR, 0.63; 95% CI, 0.039-1.00**), and rehospitalization for heart failure (**HR, 0.49; 95% CI, 0.36-0.65**) compared with transcatheter aortic valve replacement alone."
  - ★**bleeding の "95% CI, 0.039-1.00" は下限の桁が異常（0.39 の誤植の疑い）**。原文表記のまま記載。**この CI は採用しないこと**。P値の記載なし。

- 締めくくり（逐語）:
  > "consideration for a surgical treatment plan of the valve pathology, along with surgical ablation and left atrial appendage occlusion, should be part of every heart team discussion with all patients presenting with atrial fibrillation and structural heart disease."

- AAD: "Most patients undergoing surgical ablation are administered perioperative class I or III antiarrhythmic drugs, such as amiodarone, and these are often continued for **2 to 3 months** after surgical ablation. Most patients who achieve stable sinus rhythm after surgical ablation can eventually discontinue all antiarrhythmic agents."（refs 115, 180, 181）

## 8. 本レビューにとっての含意（原著の主張と読み手の推論を区別）

### 原著が明示的に主張していること
1. **AFを伴う初回・非緊急の心臓手術は、術式を問わず SA + LAAO を「両方」行うべき**（SA: 僧帽弁 I/A、非僧帽弁 I/B-NR；LAAO: 全例 I/A）。2017年版のような術式列挙は撤廃された。
2. **LAAO の I/A 格上げは LAAOS III が単独で駆動している**。しかも LAAOS III は「術後もOACを継続した上で」の上乗せ効果（stroke RR 0.66; 95% CI 0.52-0.84）であり、**全死亡は減らさなかった**ことを原文も明記している。
3. **lesion set は「より広く・より完全に」が原則**。PVI単独の rhythm success は 50-80%、full biatrial maze は >90%（refs 90, 98, 99）。LA後壁隔離の省略・不完全化、冠静脈洞ラインの欠如は失敗因子として名指しされる。LA径 ≥4.5 cm または中等度超のMRに対する PVI単独は **Class III（有害／推奨されない）**。
4. **不完全な lesion set は proarrhythmic** であり atypical macro-reentrant atrial flutter を誘発しうる。
5. **ganglionated plexi ablation は付加価値なし＋合併症増加**として、追加手技の選択肢から明確に排除された。
6. **ハイブリッドアブレーションはカテーテル単独に優越する（CEASE-AF 71% vs 39%、HARTCAP-AF/CONVERGE 89%・65% vs 41%・37%）** が、**推奨クラスは与えられていない**。stand-alone の推奨は依然 "Cox maze III/IV lesion set as the preferred procedure"（IIa/B-NR）であり、**"Epicardial ablation with atypical lesions remains exploratory"** と釘を刺している。
7. **SAの長期利益は本ガイドライン自身のプール解析で定量化されている**: 2年超で all-cause mortality HR 0.77 (0.69-0.86)、stroke HR 0.69 (0.64-0.75)、thromboembolism HR 0.68 (0.63-0.74)。**その代償が PPM: HR 1.38 (1.22-1.57)**。この「利益と代償のセット提示」は本ガイドラインの独自貢献。
8. **AFMR は "bystander" ではなく valve dysfunction の原因**であり、TEER単独は palliative、SA+LAAO を伴う外科治療が curative という立場（COR IIa/B-NR で TAVR/TEER 検討例への外科提案を推奨）。
9. **リズム成功の定義は「1年時点で AF free かつ AAD free」**、追跡は最低1年・最長5年、"at least periodic 24-hour Holter monitoring should be routine"。既存文献の多くが連続心電図モニタを用いていないことを繰り返し批判している。
10. **実施率が推奨に追いついていない**（2022年に SA+LAAO 両方は43%、無治療30%、この5年で改善なし）。

### 読み手（心臓外科医）としての推論・注意
1. **エビデンスの「新しさ」は限定的**。2017-2023年の検索で >1143 ヒットのうち **詳細解析に至ったのは新規35編のみ**。COR/LOE が実質的に動いたのは LAAO の1項目だけで、SA については文言拡張にとどまる。「I/A への格上げ」を LAAO と SA で混同しないこと。
2. **SA の長期利益を支えるのは大規模観察データとその pooled estimate であって RCT ではない**。CTSN RCT の死亡は 6.8% vs 8.7% で有意差なし。Figure 2 のプール HR 0.77 は 8 観察研究（median follow-up 2.6-6.7年）由来であり、**異質性の指標（I²）も P値も本文に提示されていない**。
3. **PPM のトレードオフは無視できない**。STS database で 7.8% vs 5.9%（OR 1.33, 1.24-1.43, P<.0001）、長期プールで HR 1.38 (1.22-1.57)、報告レンジは 1.6-14%。日本の高齢患者集団への外挿では、この幅が意思決定を左右しうる。
4. **AKI シグナル（OR 1.89, 1.12-3.18, P=.017）は単施設研究由来**で、原著も "a direct causal link ... has yet to be established" と留保している。
5. **ハイブリッド／PFA**: 本ガイドラインは **PFA（pulsed field ablation／electroporation）に一切言及していない**（全文で0ヒット）。2023年1月までの文献検索という制約による。**PFAを扱う本レビューのセクションでは、本ガイドラインを「PFA以前の到達点」として位置づける必要がある**。
6. **数値誤植が3か所ある**（後述）。統合レビューで孫引きしないこと。
7. LAAOS III の primary end point（ischemic stroke or systemic thromboembolism）の効果量そのものは本ガイドラインに記載がなく、記載されているのは stroke の RR のみ。原著（NEJM 2021）を直接参照すべき。

## 9. 本ガイドラインが触れていない／不十分な論点（★重要）

- **PFA（pulsed field ablation）・electroporation**: 言及ゼロ。
- **胸腔鏡下 stand-alone の推奨クラス**: アプローチ（thoracoscopic / robotic）そのものに独立した推奨は与えられていない。推奨文はあくまで lesion set（Cox maze III/IV）ベース。
- **ハイブリッドアブレーションに対する推奨クラス**: 本文で3つのRCTを肯定的に紹介しながら、**推奨表に項目がない**。
- **AFMR に対する外科的介入（弁形成 + Maze + LAAO）の具体的推奨・数値**: 概念的記述と TAVR/TEER 全般の IIa 推奨に含まれるのみ。AFMR 単独の推奨・エビデンスはない。
- **エネルギー源（RF vs cryo）別の推奨**: 原文は "precluding statements and recommendations that are specific to different surgical techniques" と明言し、**エネルギー源別・LAA閉鎖法別の推奨は意図的に出していない**（ただしステープラー単独の劣位は本文で警告）。
- **blanking period・>30秒閾値・AT/AFL の扱い**: 定義なし。
