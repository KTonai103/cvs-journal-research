## 4. Section 12 本文の内容（外科医にとっての実質）

### 4.1 用語・術式分類（12.1）

- 手術アブレーションを3群に分類（原文）: "Surgical ablation procedures for AF can be grouped into three different groups: (i) a full, biatrial Cox maze procedure; (ii) PVI or posterior LA isolation alone, or (iii) PVI combined with an extended LA lesion set. **All surgical AF ablation approaches are combined with LAA exclusion.**"
- **"Cox maze procedure" という用語の定義を厳格化**（原文）: "At present, it is recommended that the term 'Cox maze procedure' is appropriately used only to refer to a biatrial lesion set including specific transmural lesions that extend between non-conductive tissues (valve annulus or vena cava or another lesion; Figure 14)."（根拠: McGilvray/Damiano *JTCVS Tech* 2023）
- 原文: "The best late rhythm outcomes have been shown with the full biatrial Cox maze procedure, while a certain subgroup of patients, such as those with paroxysmal AF, have reasonable results with more limited lesion sets."
- **ABLATE trial**（bipolar RF、FDA承認の根拠、prospective multicenter non-RCT）: 非発作性AFで CABG および/または弁手術＋Cox maze IV。**"resulted in a 76% freedom from AF recurrence off AADs at 6 months with a major peri-operative adverse event rate of 9%."**（95%CI記載なし／N数の記載なし）

### 4.2 エネルギー源とデバイス（12.1.1–12.1.2）— transmurality の実データ

- 対象は RF と cryo のみ。原文: "Radiofrequency ablation and cryoablation are the two dominant technologies used today … **While there have not been any RCTs to compare the efficacy of one ablation technology with another**, these technologies have had proven clinical efficiency over the last two decades."
- **Unipolar RF**: "The unipolar devices do not provide surgeons with transmurality indicators." / "Unlike bipolar RF devices, unipolar ones have failed to consistently create transmural lesions and have a risk of forming endocardial char or thrombus." / **FDA承認なし**（"None have been FDA-approved for surgical treatment of AF."）。唯一のFDA承認 unipolar デバイスは hybrid 用の EPi-Sense（AtriCure）。
- **Bipolar clamp（Isolator Synergy, AtriCure）**: 併施心臓手術でのAF治療に**FDA承認された唯一の**デバイス。
  - 慢性動物実験では single application で全病変 transmural。
  - **ヒト ex-vivo 心臓摘出モデルでは "a single application resulted in only 65% of lesions being transmural throughout their entire length."** 非transmural化の要因は "increased thickness of atrial tissue and the presence of epicardial fat"。**"Application of two successive ablations without unclamping resulted in 100% lesion transmurality."**（95%CI・P値記載なし）
  - **irrigated bipolar RF clamp（Cardioblate BP2/LP, Medtronic）**: 同じヒトex-vivoモデルで "a double application without unclamping results in significantly increased rate of lesion transmurality compared with single application (**92 vs. 74%**)."（P値・95%CI記載なし）
  - **Isolator Synergy EnCompass Clamp（AtriCure）**: in-vivo beating heart モデルで "reliable transmurality and isolation of the entire posterior LA wall and all four PVs with a single application"。ただし "Further clinical trials will be needed to test its clinical performance."
- **臨床上の但し書き（重要）**: "In a chronic animal study using this device with a single application, all lesions produced were transmural. However, **in clinical experience, multiple applications are needed to achieve exit block.**"
- **Directional（non-clamp）デバイス**: 焼灼時間 10–40秒／回、"with the highest risk of ablation gaps at the end of the devices; thus, continuous lesions should be overlapped to increase transmurality"。Isolator pen / Coolrail は "creation of transmural lesions has been inferior to bipolar RF clamps"。**Coolrail は AEF（atrio-esophageal fistula）の症例報告があり注意**（"the Coolrail device should be used with caution as it has been associated with a few case reports of AEFs following AF ablation." 対策として "Rinsing the pericardium with saline may be used to prevent AEF"）。
- **Suction-assisted デバイス**: Cobra Fusion 150（uni+bipolar、吸引 **−500 mmHg** を1–2分維持）→ 急性ブタ拍動心モデルで "**68% rate of lesion transmurality**"。EPi-Sense（3 cm、suction、irrigated unipolar RF）は動物実験で "lesion transmurality … has been variable in multiple animal studies from **15 to 100%**"。
- **Cryo**: "cryothermy creates homogenous scars in a non-directional pattern" / 弁組織周囲に理想的（"preserves fibrous tissue and the extracellular matrix"）。欠点は "the relatively longer time to create a lesion (**2–3 min**)" と拍動心での heat sink。→ **"cryoablation should not be used to create epicardial lesions off cardiopulmonary bypass."** 均一な連続病変には **−30°C 未満**の到達が必須（"a critical lethal temperature of <−30°C must be reached during ablation"）。
  - デバイス: argon の Cardioblate CryoFlex（Medtronic、最低 **−160°C**）、nitrous oxide の cryoFORM / cryoICE（AtriCure、プローブ最低 **−50 to −70°C**）。
  - cryoICE 慢性ヒツジモデル: 心内膜側施行で "transmurality was achieved in almost all atrial lesions (**98%**)"。CryoFlex 慢性イヌモデル: LAA・PV 病変で **93%**、Cox maze 線状病変で **84%** の組織切片 transmurality。
  - **"There have been no surgical cryoablation devices that have yet received an FDA indication for the treatment of AF"**（ただし N2O・argon 両者で臨床試験進行中）。
- **RF の生物物理**: "Cardiac muscle exposed to temperature of 55°C or higher for more than a few seconds will show irreversible coagulation necrosis." / 非灌流クランプでは "Care should be taken to clean the electrodes after every two to three ablations … because char decreases conductance, which can result in non-transmural lesions. Epicardial fat and muscle thickness can also decrease conductance and limit ablation depth."

### 4.3 Lesion set（12.1.3）

- 前提（原文）: "**There are insufficient high-quality data on what should be the most important targets during surgical ablation.**"
- **PVI（12.1.3.1）**
  - "As with catheter ablation, PVI is a foundational part of all surgical AF ablation procedures. **Although documentation of exit and/or entrance block across PVs is preferred, it is infrequently performed.**"
  - 術中マッピングでは LSPAF＋僧帽弁疾患で LA/RA ともに複雑な activation → "indicating that a simplified approach with PVI alone may not be adequate during concomitant surgical ablation."
  - 低侵襲 PVI＋GP＋Marshall靭帯のみの単施設コホート: **5年での single-procedural success 37.8%**（ECG＋transtelephonic monitoring による）。
  - "Retrospective observational data suggest that surgical ablation with PVI alone is inferior to the biatrial maze procedure in patients with persistent or long-standing persistent AF."
  - 一方 CTSN（ref 1345, Gillinov 2015 系列）: "One randomized trial on non-paroxysmal AF patients undergoing mitral valve surgery reported similar rate of freedom from AF with PVI as compared to the biatrial maze procedure. **However, the study was underpowered to adequately answer the question of which lesion set was more efficacious.**"
- **LA posterior wall isolation（12.1.3.2）— 最重要**
  - "**Isolation of the entire LAPW and all four PVs is the most important part of surgical ablation procedures.**"
  - Cox maze IV の大規模後ろ向き研究（Henn 2015）: **後壁全体を isolate できなかった群は 5年で AF free off AADs が 33%、isolate した群は 66%（P = 0.017）**。（95%CI記載なし、分数の記載なし＝原文は%のみ）
  - 僧帽弁手術例ではさらに影響大: "the failure to isolate the entire posterior LA during a Cox maze procedure was **the only independent predictor of procedural failure** and resulted in **6.7-fold increased risk of AF recurrence**."（95%CI・P値記載なし）
  - 結論（原文）: "Due to anticipated improvement in rhythm outcome with LA PWI, **a minimum of PVI plus LA PWI should be performed in patients undergoing surgical AF ablation.**"
- **RA/LA linear lesions（12.1.3.3）**
  - CTI（右）と mitral isthmus（左）は macroreentrant AT 予防が目的。
  - 胸腔鏡下アブレーションの単施設連続例で "adjunctive CTI ablation significantly increased freedom from atrial tachyarrhythmia recurrence."（効果量・95%CI・P値いずれも記載なし）
  - **"The superior-inferior vena cava ablation line anchors the RA isthmus line and thus serves an important role in preventing late RA flutter. Documentation of completeness is beneficial in all deployed linear lesions during surgical AF ablation."**
- **Ganglionated plexi（12.1.3.4）— 明確な否定**
  - "The only randomized study examining the efficacy and safety of additional GP ablation in patients undergoing thoracoscopic surgery showed **no incremental benefit in AF recurrence rate and a significantly higher rate of major procedural complications** in patients randomized to GP ablation."（AFACT試験。ref 1008。効果量・95%CI・P値の記載なし）
  - **"Therefore, with the exception of a clinical trial setting, GP ablation should not be performed during surgical AF ablation."**（推奨表には項目化されていない、本文中の記述）
  - 別箇所（本文 Section 8 系）でも: "In the AFACT study, adjunctive epicardial GP ablation during thoracoscopic AF surgery did not improve freedom from AF recurrence but was associated with an increased risk of major [complications]"
- **Ligament of Marshall（12.1.3.5）**: "**There are no data from the surgical literature to support the ligament of Marshall as a target for ablation.** However, this structure is usually divided while isolating the left PVs during surgical ablation procedures."
- **LAA exclusion（12.1.3.6）**: "Exclusion of the LAA is a standard part of all surgical AF ablation procedures"（詳細は §4.7）。

### 4.4 Cox maze IV lesion set（Figure 14 の図説、原文逐語）

(A) Left atrial lesion set including: (i) left atriotomy, (ii) ablation around the left-sided PVs, (iii) ablation around the right-sided PVs, (iv) posterior wall box lesion, (v) line connecting left PV lesion to excluded LAA, (vi) line connecting box lesion to mitral annulus, (vii) cryoablation to the epicardial ostial region of the coronary sinus (not shown). ### 4.5 併施（concomitant）手術（12.2）

**有効性（12.2.1）**
- "Concomitant AF surgery has been shown to increase SR maintenance rate in multiple randomized and non-randomized trials."
- **23 RCT のメタ解析**: "A metaanalysis of 23 RCTs demonstrated that AF ablation concomitant to cardiac surgery results in **increased freedom from AF at 12 months**."（効果量・95%CI・P値いずれも記載なし＝**原文に数値なし**）
- 脳卒中: "Several trials have demonstrated a **reduced incidence of stroke at 5 years** postoperatively."（数値記載なし）
- **長期生存**: "**Improvement of long-term survival after concomitant surgical AF ablation has not been proved by an RCT.**" ただし STS AF database の propensity matching で **30日死亡率の低下**と関連（数値記載なし）。さらに "several retrospective and propensity-matched studies as well as large national registries have demonstrated that the performance of surgical AF ablation concomitant with other cardiac procedures (**particularly mitral valve surgery and CABG**) was associated with improved long-term survival."
- **isolated CABG**: "In a retrospective propensity score–matched analysis of a nationwide registry, concomitant surgical ablation for AF in patients undergoing **isolated CABG** was shown to significantly improve long-term survival rates."（Suwalski 2020 EJCTS。数値記載なし）
- QoL: "Improved QoL at a long-term postoperative follow-up period has also been demonstrated in patients who underwent AF surgery with SR restoration."（数値記載なし）

**安全性（12.2.2）**
- "Several studies, including RCTs, have demonstrated that **concomitant AF surgery is safe and does not increase operative mortality.**"
- AKI: "Although a propensity score–matching study showed an increased incidence of **acute kidney injury** after AF surgery, the associated long-term risks were offset by the significant survival benefit derived from the concomitant Cox maze procedure."（数値記載なし）
- **術後 AT**: "Incomplete linear lesions with residual conduction and inappropriate surgical techniques are mainly responsible for postoperative occurrence of predominantly macroreentrant ATs. **Intraoperative verification of conduction block, particularly to ensure PVI, may reduce the incidence of AT due to incomplete ablation.**"
- **ペースメーカ**: "Increased incidence of new permanent pacemaker implantation after the Cox maze procedure has been demonstrated in many studies. **However, in a recent large European registry of patients undergoing valve surgery, surgical AF ablation was not associated with increased need for permanent pacemaker implantation.**" / "**Sinus node dysfunction requiring permanent pacemaker implantation can occur in up to 10% of patients after the Cox maze procedure for non-paroxysmal AF** and may be a result of unmasking preexisting sick sinus syndrome." / 術中要因として "mechanical or thermal injury to the sinus or AV node and interruption of the conduction system arterial supply"。
- 対策（原文）: "**Multidisciplinary collaboration between cardiothoracic surgeons and electrophysiologists, proper training on ablation techniques, and deployment of complete linear lesions** may reduce the incidence of postoperative atrial tachyarrhythmias and permanent pacemaker implantation and enhance patient outcomes."

**LA open 症例の lesion set（12.2.3）**
- "**The biatrial Cox maze procedure is the preferred procedure for surgical AF ablation during open LA procedures** and achieves high rates of AF conversion to SR and freedom from AF recurrence. However, recognizing that surgical training and experience may vary across centers, the writing group suggests that **biatrial Cox maze procedure or a minimum of PVI plus LA PWI is required** in patients undergoing surgical ablation concomitant to LA open cardiac surgery."

**non-LA open（closed）症例の lesion set（12.2.4）**
- 実施率が低い理由: "because of the necessity of adding an LA incision to perform ablation in the LA"。
- "**Epicardial PVI alone has been performed more often than a biatrial maze procedure** in AF patients undergoing non-LA open procedures, and **this might have led to biased analyses of the data.**"
- "A dilated LA has been shown to be associated with worse AF-free and event-free survivals after PVI for patients with paroxysmal AF undergoing non-LA open cardiac surgery."
- "Several studies have shown that **biatrial maze procedure is associated with superior rhythm outcome and a lower risk of adverse events and long-term overall mortality compared with LA lesion sets** following surgical AF ablation concomitant to non-mitral valve surgery."（数値記載なし）
- ただし現実論: "**However, many surgeons are reluctant to increase procedural complexity and risks by performing AF ablation through an atriotomy.** Therefore, selection of PVI combined with PWI or other modified procedure **should be individualized** in patients undergoing surgical ablation concomitant to closed (non-LA open) cardiac surgery. **Further clinical studies are needed to clarify the optimal lesion set in these patients** and to further elucidate associated risks and benefits."

**Stand-alone Cox maze（12.3.1）**

| MacGregor 2022（ref 1323、最大シリーズ） | **236 consecutive patients** | 59% prior failed catheter ablation、**median duration of preoperative AF 6.2 years**（IQR記載なし） | "**89 and 77% freedom from recurrent atrial tachyarrhythmias at 5 and 10 years**, respectively, irrespective of AF type or surgical approach (median sternotomy vs. minimally invasive approach)" | "without 30-day mortality" |

**低侵襲・hybrid の総論（12.3.2）**
- 比較の限界（原文）: "comparison of different study results is limited by **non-uniformity in patient populations and lack of standardized surgical technique, ablation technology and deployed lesion sets.**"
- hybrid の本質（原文）: "A key aspect of this treatment strategy is that it **harmonizes epicardial and endocardial ablation components** to effectively target key drivers of AF, including the PVs and the LAPW."
- **single-stage vs dual-stage**: "In the single-stage model, epicardial and endocardial procedures can occur back-to-back in the same suite or separate suites or over sequential days. **Completion of both phases in the same session prolongs procedural time, which may add additional risk in patients with comorbidities.** For dual-staged programs, the epicardial component typically occurs in the cardiac operating room, and the endocardial component is scheduled **~2–4 months later** in the electrophysiology laboratory, aiming to complement the epicardial component with touch-up lesions, ensuring isolation of PVs, LAPW, and completeness of epicardially deployed linear lesions. **The impact of different procedural timing on patient outcomes is unknown.**"

**胸腔鏡下アプローチ（12.3.2.1）— 手技詳細（外科医向け）**
- 全身麻酔＋ダブルルーメンチューブによる分離換気。
- **右側ポート**: "a camera port is placed in the **fifth intercostal space midaxillary line**, a **5 or 10 mm working port in the sixth or seventh intercostal space anterior axillary line**, and a **5 mm working port in the third intercostal space anterior axillary line**. **The pericardium is opened anterior to the PN**（横隔神経の前方）. Blunt dissection is used to open the **transverse and oblique sinuses**. Antral isolation of the right PVs **as a pair** is performed with repetitive applications using a bipolar RF clamp"
- **左側**: "The same port incisions are made on the left side but placed **more posteriorly**. **The pericardium is opened posterior to the PN.**"
- **重症COPD例の工夫**: "thoracoscopic epicardial isolation of the PVs can be performed **only on the left**, and the right PVs subsequently isolated **from the endocardium** to avoid bilateral sequential lung deflation. An alternative would also be a **convergent procedure using subxiphoid access along with Lariat closure of the LAA.**"
- **box作成**: "After PVI documentation, a **roof line (connecting both superior PVs)** and an **inferior line (connecting both inferior PVs)** are made epicardially using directional ablation devices … to create box isolation of the LAPW (Figure 15). As an alternative, **one epicardial box lesion including the posterior LA wall and the PVs can be created using only the irrigated bipolar biparietal Cardioblate Gemini-S (Medtronic Inc.) RF ablation system by performing two clamp lesions from the right and left sides that overlap in the middle of the posterior wall.**"
- 追加病変: "circular lesion of the SVC, linear lesion connecting both caval veins and mitral isthmus line. Endocardial touch-up ablation to achieve bidirectional block can be delivered in the same or subsequent stage with catheter ablation. Furthermore, in patients with prior history or intraprocedural induction of CTI-dependent flutter, the CTI is ablated endocardially."
- **LAA（重要）**: "**Left atrial appendage exclusion should also be performed in all patients during minimally invasive surgical AF ablation to reduce thromboembolic risk.** Through an incision at the **third to fourth intercostal space** or via a completely thoracoscopic approach, the surgeon can easily reach the LAA and exclude it. **Left atrial appendage exclusion must be performed at the very base of the structure, as to avoid leaving behind residual stump. Incomplete removal of the LAA is associated with increased risk of local thrombus formation and embolization.**"
- **抗凝固不要という独自の適応**: "Since the thoracoscopic surgical AF ablation lesions are **exclusively epicardial**, one can perform the procedure **without the need for anticoagulation during and after the procedure**. Therefore, stand-alone thoracoscopic surgical AF ablation is **the best option for patients who have bleeding diathesis (particularly central nervous system bleeds)** precluding the anticoagulation needed during and after endocardial catheter ablation. Additional suitable patient subsets include those **without the ability to achieve LA access endocardially (large atrial septal occlusion device, interrupted inferior vena cava)** or those with **LAA thrombus**."
- Figure 15 図説（原文）: "Posterior view of the left atrium showing epicardial lesion sets during thoracoscopic surgical AF ablation: pulmonary vein isolation with connecting roof and inferior lines (A), en-bloc pulmonary vein and posterior wall isolation using the Cardioblate Gemini-S (Medtronic Inc.) RF ablation system (B), and posterior wall ablation using the convergent approach (C)."

**Hybrid convergent（12.3.2.2）**
- 沿革: "first described in 2009, and clinical outcomes were published in 2010 by Kiser et al."
- 手技: "minimally invasive, **closed-chest procedure performed on the beating heart** that combines epicardial RF ablation focused on the LAPW followed by complementary endocardial catheter ablation. Epicardial ablation is performed under endoscopic visualization using a **closed-irrigation, unipolar RF catheter device (EPi-Sense Guided Coagulation System with Visitrax, AtriCure, Inc.)**. The device is inserted via a **small subxiphoid incision using a pericardioscopic cannula (Subtle, AtriCure, Inc.)**"
- 通電条件: "**Each lesion is created by a 90 s application of alternating current via an impedance-based power control algorithm with a maximum output of 30 W.** Lesions are overlapped across the entire LAPW to promote contiguity and thus minimize gaps."
- 到達範囲の限界: "**The epicardial stage aims to debulk as much of the LAPW as can be accessed and is limited at the superior margin of the lesion set by the oblique sinus.** Posterior segments of the PV ostia/antra may also be reached and ablated in most cases."
- **胸腔鏡下との決定的差異（原文）**: "**A key difference between thoracoscopic ablation and the hybrid convergent procedure is that PVI is performed epicardially using bipolar RF clamps in the former, while in the latter, it is achieved endocardially with catheter ablation.**"
- 成績（観察研究の幅）: "Single-center and multicenter studies have reported **66–95% freedom from atrial tachyarrhythmias at 1 year** following the hybrid convergent procedure, with **52–81% arrhythmia-free survival without the use of AADs.**"
- **合併症と対策（外科医に有用）**: 食道熱傷は "careful device orientation, esophageal temperature monitoring, and prophylactic irrigation of the pericardial space" で回避。**Dressler症候群による遅発性心嚢液貯留・タンポナーデ**は "pericardial drains and prophylactic medications (colchicine, steroids, and/or nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs)" で予防。"Timely diagnosis of pericardial effusion is facilitated by patient education on symptoms and **transthoracic echo surveillance at ~2–4 weeks**." / "greater experience has been associated with reduced procedural complications."
- 発展: 大規模研究で endocardial cryoballoon 併用の報告あり。"The availability of **PFA** can improve safety and durability of endocardial lesions delivered at the posterior wall and thus potentially increase success rates."（**PFA は "potentially" の推測記述であり、hybrid における PFA の臨床成績データは本文に示されていない**）。LAA については "limited studies have also combined the hybrid convergent approach with epicardial placement of a **clip or stapled excision of the LAA** reporting favourable initial results."

### 4.8 Left atrial appendage exclusion（12.4）

- 前提（原文）: "**The LAA is the site of thrombus location in 90% of non-rheumatic AF patients with stroke** and is a well-documented target for stroke reduction in patients with AF."
- Cox maze III の初期評価で "a reduction in late stroke after surgery" が示唆され、後続の後ろ向きシリーズでも "a lower-than-expected incidence of late neurologic events after a Cox maze procedure, **independent of the preoperative CHA2DS2-VASc score or long-term warfarin use**"。**"The reduction in stroke has been attributed to both SR restoration and LAA elimination."**
- **手技別の評価（重要）**: "Historically, the most common techniques for exclusion of the LAA have been **internal ligation, excision, or stapling at the base**. **Unfortunately, the efficacy of internal ligation and stapled excision or exclusion have been poor in late follow-up.** While surgical excision has been shown to be effective, there has been **concern for bleeding complications, especially in elderly patients with friable tissue.** A more recent technique has been the use of **external clips placed either under direct visualization or thoracoscopically at the base of the appendage (AtriClip, AtriCure, Inc.)**."
- **AtriClip のエビデンス**
  - FDA承認: 第1世代 **2009年**（開心術併施でのLAA閉鎖）、**Pro-V が2016年、FLEX-V が2018年**に承認。
  - **EXCLUDE trial**（large prospective non-randomized）: "**60 of 61 patients** had a successful LAA exclusion at the **3-month** follow-up with a first-generation AtriClip device."（**分数が原文どおり記載されている**）
  - 長期: "the long-term results from a prospective device trial reported that **all 36 patients were without stroke, and there was 100% LAA occlusion confirmed by imaging at 3 years without thrombi, reperfusion, or residual neck stump of >1 cm.**"
  - 大規模シリーズ: "In a recent larger series of **291 patients** undergoing epicardial deployment of AtriClip device during open-heart surgery, **the LAA was successfully excluded at 3 years in all patients.** Furthermore, the subgroup of patients with LAA occlusion who **discontinued OAC** during follow-up had a **87.5% relative risk reduction in ischemic stroke** when compared with the **expected rate** in patients with similar CHA2DS2-VASc score."（⚠ 対照は実測群ではなく **expected rate**。95%CI・P値記載なし）
  - stand-alone: "Several studies have established the safety and long-term efficacy of **stand-alone minimally invasive or thoracoscopic LAA occlusion with the AtriClip** device in patients who either **cannot be anticoagulated** or who are **not candidates for a transcatheter approach**."
- **LAAOS III（ref 1335）— 併施LAA閉鎖の最良エビデンス**
  - 原文: "The role of concomitant surgical LAA occlusion, **in addition to OAC use**, is best supported by a large RCT on LAA occlusion (LAAOS III). **Over 4800 patients** with AF undergoing cardiac surgery were randomized to LAA occlusion (**amputation, stapling, or suturing**) or no treatment and were followed for a **mean period of 3.8 years**, with **76.8% of the participants continuing their OAC treatment**. **At 3 years, there were significantly fewer strokes and systemic emboli in the occluded as compared to the non-occluded cohort (4.8 vs. 7%, P = 0.001).**"
  - **95%CI・HRの記載なし**（本コンセンサス本文では効果量はイベント率の対比とP値のみ）。分数の記載なし（原文は%のみ）。

## 5. 外科に関係する周辺章（S1として押さえるべき定義・判定基準）

### 5.1 AF分類（Table 4、原文逐語）— 試験比較の前提
- "**Paroxysmal**—continuous AF episode lasting longer than 30 s but terminating spontaneously or with intervention within 7 days of onset"
  - "**Early paroxysmal**—continuous AF episode lasting longer than 30 s but terminating spontaneously or with intervention within 24 h of onset"
- "**Persistent**—continuous AF episode lasting longer than 7 days but <1 year"
  - "**Early persistent**—continuous AF episode lasting longer than 7 days but <3 months"
- "**Long-standing persistent**—continuous AF episode lasting longer than 1 year, in whom rhythm control management is being pursued"
- "**Permanent**—AF for which a therapeutic decision has been made not to pursue sinus rhythm restoration"
- 注記（原文）: "permanent AF represents a **therapeutic attitude** on the part of a patient and the treating physician rather than on any inherent pathophysiological attribute of the AF."
- **early paroxysmal / early persistent は本コンセンサスで導入された細分化**（AHA/ACC/HRS・CCS・CSANZ・ESC の duration-based 分類に "slight modifications" を加えたもの）。

### 5.2 リズム判定 ★（Section 9.4, 9.5 — 外科試験の解釈に直結）

- **無症候性再発の多さ（原文逐語）**: "In the CIRCA-DOSE trial, **only 45% of the symptom-triggered activations were adjudicated as AFl or AF** during the postablation continuous monitoring of paroxysmal AF patients using an ICM."
- "Two studies reported that the proportion of asymptomatic AF events was **11–35% prior to and 53–65% after ablation**."
- **DISCERN-AF**: "the ratio of asymptomatic to symptomatic AF episodes significantly increased from **1.1 before to 3.7 after ablation** with **12% of patients having asymptomatic recurrences only**."
- **CIRCA-DOSE**: "the **1-year arrhythmia-free survival** based on the presence of documented recurrence of either symptomatic or asymptomatic atrial tachyarrhythmia lasting **>30 s on continuous cardiac monitoring was 52.6%**, while the respective survival free from **symptomatic only** arrhythmia recurrences was **85.3%**."
  → **モニタリング強度で成功率が 52.6% ↔ 85.3% と33ポイント動く**。外科シリーズの「73%」「89%」等を読むときの必須の割引率。
- **試験設定でのモニタリング推奨（原文逐語）**: "the writing group suggests that, **in the clinical trial setting and in the absence of invasive monitoring, a minimum of 24-hour continuous Holter type monitor should be considered every 3 months for the first year following catheter ablation, preferably in combination with symptom-based monitoring. Where available, longer duration recordings with 7-day or 14-day continuous monitoring are preferable.**"
- **AF burden の妥当な評価**: "A reasonable assessment of true AF burden is achieved by **at least 28 days of annual cumulative intermittent non-invasive monitoring using serial longer term (7-day and 14-day) ambulatory ECG devices.**" / "commonly employed short-duration (24 and 48 h) ambulatory monitors **may overestimate** the true AF burden."

### 5.3 Blanking period（9.5.2）— **本改訂の重要変更点**
- 原文: "**With the current evidence, a consensus among the members of this writing group has been reached to recommend an 8-week blanking period after AF ablation.** This cutoff was agreed while placing emphasis to minimize misclassification of patients with early recurrences that are not indicative of treatment failure and their pertinent exposure to an unnecessary need for redo ablation procedure. **The writing group supports the use of the revised 8-week blanking period in future clinical trial design.**"
- 根拠となる数値（いずれも原文どおり）:
  - Calkins ら: "patients free from AF recurrence during the 3-month blanking period have **90% likelihood** of remaining free from AF recurrence at a 12-month follow-up or longer (**89% negative predictive value for paroxysmal and 91% for persistent AF**)."
  - ADVICE trial（401例、発作性AF、transtelephonic monitoring 12か月）: 早期再発 44.6%。"One year freedom from AF recurrence was **77.2%** in patients **without** early recurrence compared with **62.6, 36.4 and 7.8%** in patients with early recurrence in the **first, second, and third month** after ablation, respectively."
  - CIRCA-DOSE 事前規定サブ解析: "occurrences of early recurrence in the first, second, and third month of blanking period were associated with **4.9, 26.8, and 63.4 times higher likelihood of late recurrence**"（95%CI・P値記載なし）。"Early recurrences occurring later than **52 days** following catheter ablation had a **95% specificity** for predicting late recurrence."
  - 早期再発の報告頻度は "**ranges from 16 to 67%**"、CIRCA-DOSE では連続モニタで **61%**（初回早期再発までの **median interval 12 days**、IQR記載なし）。
- 再アブレーション: "the writing group suggests that **reablation of atrial tachyarrhythmia recurrences within the blanking period is not** [recommended]"（原文がこの行で切れており、続きは未確認）

### 5.4 「成功」の定義（>30秒）に対するこの文書の立場（Section 9末尾）
- 原文逐語: "Since the first AF ablation consensus statement published in 2007, AF ablation success has been defined in a dichotomous manner by **the absence of any atrial arrhythmia lasting >30 s off AADs**. **Overwhelming evidence indicates that this 30 s cutoff does not correlate with symptom severity, is not associated with cardiovascular outcomes, and results in marked underestimation of treatment efficacy.**"
- CIRCA-DOSE 二次解析: "**a 1 h duration threshold** of postablation AF recurrence is associated with subsequent patient clinical outcome, since longer AF recurrences resulted in significantly increased healthcare utilization and impaired disease-specific QoL."
- 現時点の方針（原文）: "**we continue to recommend reporting the 30 s threshold data to allow comparison with earlier literature.** Furthermore, it seems rational to move towards **reporting AF burden** to define ablation outcomes in a more granular fashion … **It also remains important to report all categories of recurrence transparently, such as freedom from symptomatic atrial arrhythmias, AF recurrence separately from other atrial arrhythmias, single and multiple procedure success rates, and success on and off antiarrhythmic therapy. Success rate should be reported at 1 year and after single and multiple procedures.**"

### 5.5 Posterior wall isolation のカテーテル側エビデンス（Section 8.2.5）— 外科の PWI 推奨との対比に必須
- **CAPLA**: "338 patients with symptomatic persistent AF undergoing first-time RF ablation were randomized to either PVI (wide antral circumferential) plus PWI (roof and floor line deployment plus ablation of earliest electrograms within the box if needed) or PVI alone. **Contact force–sensing catheters were used** with specific lesion quality targets, and the follow-up monitoring was intense (**twice daily ECG transmissions**). **There was no difference in the primary study endpoint with 53.3% freedom from AF at 12 months in the PVI group as compared to 54.1% in the PVI + PWI group.**"
- **POBI-AF**: 217例、持続性AF、PVI vs PVI＋posterior box（voltage abatement <0.1 mV、roof line 双方向ブロック、entrance・exit block 記録）。box群の69%に anterior line も追加。"Using intermittent Holter monitoring, the reported freedom from any documented AF without AADs was **similar** in the PVI alone and the PVI plus PWI groups."
- Yu ら: 113例、"no improvement in outcome"。
- **カテーテルでPWIが効かない理由として挙げられている4点（原文）**: (i) "the significant variation in thickness of the **septopulmonary bundle**"、(ii) "difficulties in achieving transmural lesion at the LAPW roof **due to insulation of the epicardial muscular bundle by fat inter-position**"、(iii) "a tendency to **lower the power and duration** of energy delivery during LAPW ablation to prevent thermal injury to the neighbouring esophagus"。さらに "**LAPW reconnection rates have been reported to be as high as 40–100%**, with predominantly posterior location"。
- 代替解釈（原文）: "(i) PVI alone using a wide antral isolation strategy already encompasses much of the LAPW … (ii) the contribution of the posterior LA to persistent AF mechanism is not universal … or (iii) **survival of epicardial LAPW tissue**."
- CAPLA サブ解析: "patients with **short cycle length posterior LA activity** did derive a benefit from PWI"。
- **執筆委員のサーベイ結果**: "15.8% of the members perform PWI during first-time and 26.3% when performing redo ablation of **paroxysmal AF**. Furthermore, **31.6% of the writing group members perform PWI during first-time and 65.8% when performing redo ablation of persistent AF.**"
- **PersAFOne（PFAでのPWI）**: "PFA ablation under ICE guidance resulted in low-voltage posterior wall homogeneity with first pass in **all 24 patients** without primary safety events. … invasive remapping **2–3 months** postablation demonstrated **no evidence of conduction through the posterior wall in 100% of patients** and partial voltage recovery of the PW-ablated area in **3 of 21 patients**."（分数が原文どおり）

## 6. 前版（2017年版）からの変更点

**注意: 本文書には "What's new" / "changes from the previous document" という独立した章・表は存在しない**（grep で該当なし）。以下は**本文の記述から直接読み取れる変更点のみ**を挙げる（推論部分は明示）。

1. **Class/LOE 体系を廃し、"Category of advice"（Advice TO DO / May be appropriate TO DO / Area of uncertainty / Advice NOT TO DO）＋ "Type of evidence"（META / RAND / OBS / OPN）に変更**（Table 1・Table 2 として明記）。
2. **Blanking period を 3か月から 8週間に変更**（原文が "the **revised** 8-week blanking period" と明記）。
3. **AF分類に "early paroxysmal"（<24 h）と "early persistent"（7日〜<3か月）を追加**（"with slight modifications" と明記）。
4. **"Cox maze procedure" の用語を biatrial lesion set に限定するよう明記**（"At present, it is recommended that the term 'Cox maze procedure' is appropriately used only to refer to…"）。
5. **成功指標として AF burden の報告を推奨**（"it seems rational to move towards reporting AF burden"）。30秒閾値は「過去文献との比較のため継続報告」という位置づけに格下げ。
6. **GP ablation を明確に非推奨**（"with the exception of a clinical trial setting, GP ablation should not be performed during surgical AF ablation"）。AFACT の結果を根拠に。
7. **LAA閉鎖を stand-alone / concomitant いずれの surgical AF ablation でも "Advice TO DO"（RAND）に位置づけ**。LAAOS III（2021）の登場が反映されている。
8. **hybrid（convergent／胸腔鏡下）を独立の推奨項目として持続性AFに "May be appropriate TO DO" で明記**。CONVERGE（2021）・HARTCAP-AF・CEASE-AF が根拠として本文に収載。

## 7. 本レビュー（Maze/外科的AFアプローチ）にとっての含意

### 【原著の主張として明確に書かれていること】
1. **LA open（僧帽弁など）症例では、併施AFアブレーションは AAD の使用歴を問わず "Advice TO DO"（META）**。これはAF外科にとって最も強い推奨。
2. **closed（non-LA open：AVR・CABG 単独など）症例では、AAD 抵抗・不耐例で "Advice TO DO"、AAD 開始前の症例では "May be appropriate TO DO"** と一段階の差がつけられている。＝**LA切開を追加する負担が推奨強度に反映されている**。
3. **lesion set は「biatrial Cox maze、少なくとも PVI＋LA PWI」が LA open 症例で "Advice TO DO"（RAND）。PVI 単独は不十分**とする立場。Henn 2015 の「後壁を全部 isolate しなければ5年 33% vs 66%（P = 0.017）」が最も強い数値根拠。
4. **LAA 閉鎖は stand-alone / concomitant の別を問わず "Advice TO DO"（RAND、LAAOS III を含む）**。かつ **「基部で（at the very base）」「stump を残さない」** ことが明記され、**internal ligation・stapled excision/exclusion は late follow-up で成績不良**と断言されている。AtriClip が推奨されるべき手技として実質的に前面に出ている。
5. **exit/entrance block と線状病変完全性の術中確認は "Advice TO DO" だが Type of evidence は OPN（専門家意見）** ＝ 「やるべきだがエビデンスは弱い」。かつ実態として "**it is infrequently performed**"。
6. **GP ablation は臨床試験以外では行うべきでない**（AFACT）。
7. **stand-alone 手術／hybrid は「持続性AF＋カテーテル既治療」で May be appropriate、「発作性AF＋カテーテル既治療」では Area of uncertainty**。＝ **発作性AFの stand-alone 外科は推されていない**。
8. **hybrid の合併症率は 8–20%** と明示され、これがhybrid普及の最大の制約（"A limitation of hybrid AF ablation that restrains its wider applicability is the higher rate of complications"）。

### 【読み手（心臓外科医）としての推論・注意】
9. **transmurality は「デバイス性能」ではなく「手技の反復回数」で決まる**という点が、実験データで一貫している（bipolar clamp 単発 65% → double 100%、irrigated bipolar 74% → 92%）。「1回クランプで transmural」は動物実験の話で、**ヒト心房・心外膜脂肪ではそうならない**という記述は日常手技への直接の警告。**これは原著が示した実験値からの読み手の運用上の推論。**
10. **カテーテル側では PWI の RCT（CAPLA・POBI-AF）が軒並みnegativeなのに、外科側では PWI が必須とされている**という非対称がある。本文はその理由を「カテーテルでは LAPW の durable isolation が達成できない（reconnection 40–100%、septopulmonary bundle の厚み、脂肪、食道回避のための出力低下）」で説明しており、**外科（クランプ／box）の優位性の論拠を文書自身が提供している**と読める。**これは読み手の統合的推論であり、原著が「だから外科PWIが優れる」と直接主張しているわけではない。**
11. **成績数値の比較は必ずモニタリング強度をそろえて読む必要がある**。CIRCA-DOSE の 52.6%（連続モニタ）vs 85.3%（症状のみ）は、Ad 2017 の「5年 73%」や MacGregor 2022 の「5年 89%」が ILR ベースでない限り**過大評価されている可能性**を示唆する。CASA-AF（ILR、12か月で外科26%・カテーテル28%）はその極端な対照例。**これは読み手の推論。**
12. **8週 blanking period への変更は、今後の外科系試験設計にも波及する**（原文は "future clinical trial design" と述べ、対象をカテーテルに限定していない）。
13. **Adiyaman 2018（52例、PVI only ＋ LAA ligation の胸腔鏡）で外科が劣る（29% vs 56%）** のは、外科側の lesion set が PVI 単独だったこと（＝本コンセンサス自身が不十分と断じる lesion set）と整合する。**外科手技の質・lesion set が結果を左右することの実例として使える。これは読み手の推論。**
14. **CEASE-AF の「カテーテル群の 40.2% しか posterior wall ablation を受けていない」「11.5% しか repeat ablation を受けていない」** は、hybrid 優位（71.6% vs 39.2%）の解釈に対する重要な留保。CONVERGE についても本文自身が同型の限界（対照群に empirical PWI なし）を明記している。
15. **本文書は電気生理側4学会の合同文書であり、STS/EACTS/AATS の外科系ガイドライン（2017 STS: Badhwar / Ad らの Expert consensus は ref 1337 として引用のみ）とは別系統**。外科向けレビューでは両者の推奨強度の差を対比すると価値が出る。

## 8. 注意 — **この文書が触れていない論点**（本レビューのテーマとの gap）

1. **AFMR（atrial functional mitral regurgitation）は完全に非収載。** 文書全体で "regurgitation" という語が **0回**（grep で確認）。僧帽弁逆流・三尖弁逆流とAFの関係、AFMRに対するアブレーション／弁手術の是非は一切論じられていない。**AFMR の章を書く際、本文書は根拠として使えない。**
2. **surgical PFA（外科用パルスフィールド）についての記載は事実上なし。** PFA は Section 6.2.3 等でカテーテル用エネルギー源として詳述されるが、**Section 12（外科）でのPFA記載は hybrid convergent の endocardial 側について "The availability of PFA can improve safety and durability of endocardial lesions delivered at the posterior wall and thus potentially increase success rates." の1文のみ**で、しかも将来形の推測。**外科用PFAクランプ／プローブの臨床データは本文書にない。**
3. **Amaze trial（ref 1310）は引用番号としてのみ登場し、結果の記述がない。**
4. **併施AFアブレーション後の抗凝固中止基準が外科文脈で示されていない。** Section 9.3 の抗凝固に関する推奨はカテーテルアブレーション後を前提（"Discontinuation of anticoagulation may be reasonable 12 months following catheter ablation…"）。**LAA閉鎖後・Cox maze後のOAC中止については、AtriClip 291例シリーズの記述（OAC中止群で87.5%相対リスク減、ただし対照は expected rate）以外に推奨がない。**
5. **心房収縮能（atrial transport function）・LA機能の回復に関する定量データがない。** "Surgical ablation of AF combined with LAA exclusion or excision restores SR and atrial contraction" と述べるのみで数値なし。
6. **手術アブレーションのタイミング（AF罹病期間との関係）、LA径のカットオフ、術前予測因子について、外科特有の推奨がない。** LA径については "A dilated LA has been shown to be associated with worse AF-free and event-free survivals after PVI for patients with paroxysmal AF undergoing non-LA open cardiac surgery" の1文のみでカットオフなし。
7. **併施AFアブレーションの「洞調律維持そのものが予後を改善するか」は未解決と明言**（"Improvement of long-term survival after concomitant surgical AF ablation **has not been proved by an RCT**"）。観察研究・レジストリのみ。
8. **右房病変（RA lesion set）の必要性についての独立した推奨がない。** 12.1.3.3 で "important role in preventing late RA flutter" と述べるのみで、推奨表には RA 病変単独の項目がない（推奨No.3 は "biatrial Cox maze **or** a minimum of PVI plus LA PWI" と、両者を選択肢として並列に置いている）。
9. **エネルギー源間の比較RCTが存在しないことを明言**（"there have not been any RCTs to compare the efficacy of one ablation technology with another"）。RF vs cryo の優劣は決着していない。
10. **小児・先天性心疾患、心臓移植後、LVAD/MCS 患者のAF外科は非収載。**
