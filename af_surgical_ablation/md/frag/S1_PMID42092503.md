## 1. 文書の位置づけ

- **発行主体**: American Association for Thoracic Surgery (AATS)。AATS Adult Cardiac Clinical Practice Standards Committee と AATS Board of Directors の承認を経て投稿された。
  - "The panel and the AATS Adult Cardiac Clinical Practice Standards Committee reviewed and approved the document before it was submitted for final approval to the AATS Board of Directors and submitted for publication."
- **文書型**: Expert Consensus Document（ガイドラインではなくコンセンサス・ステートメント。ただし CoR/LoE を自ら付与している＝**一次資料としての推奨クラス提示あり**）。
- **対象疾患**: AFMR（atrial functional mitral regurgitation）。**「Maze手術そのもののガイドライン」ではない**が、Section 3 に外科的AFアブレーション・lesion set・LAA閉鎖の推奨が独立ステートメントとして含まれる。
- **本文書が明示的に扱わない領域（原文明記）**:
  - "How to manage patients with severe atrial functional TR and atrial fibrillation without significant MR is not addressed in this Consensus Statement document. - **AFMR の定義（本文書採用＝JACC: Cardiovascular Imaging Expert Panel 定義）**:
  - preserved LVEF (≥50%)、regional wall motion abnormality なし、leaflet tethering なし
  - LV 非拡大: LV end-diastolic volume ≤78 mL/m² (women), ≤85 mL/m² (men)
  - mitral annular dilatation: systolic anteroposterior diameter >35 mm
  - LA enlargement: LA volume index >40 mL/m²
  - 原文: "The above definition (ie, The Journal of the American College of Cardiology definition) was proposed by the JACC: Cardiovascular Imaging Expert Panel and was found to have the highest association with survival outcome among various definitions currently used."

## 4. 外科的AFアブレーションに関する推奨（Section 3、逐語＋根拠を全て）

### Statement 3.4 — 同時外科的AFアブレーション（CoR I, LoE B-nonrandomized）★本レビュー最重要

> "Statement 3.4: For patients undergoing surgical therapy for AFMR with atrial fibrillation, it is recommended that they undergo correction of MR with concomitant surgical atrial fibrillation ablation. (CoR I, LoE B-nonrandomized)."

本文の根拠（原文どおり）:
- "In general, patients with mitral valve disease and atrial fibrillation have the best clinical outcomes if they undergo concomitant operative treatment for both conditions."
- "Evidence from degenerative MR demonstrates that concomitant mitral valve repair and surgical atrial fibrillation ablation results in improved long-term rhythm control and survival compared with mitral valve surgery alone."（引用: ref 87 = Gillinov AM, et al. *Surgical ablation of atrial fibrillation during mitral-valve surgery.* N Engl J Med. 2015;372(15):1399-1409 [CTSN trial]、ref 88 = Lee R, McCarthy PM, et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg. 2012;143(6):1341-1351）
- **AFMR 患者における AF 合併率**: "About 60% to 80% of patients with AFMR undergoing surgical treatment have coexisting atrial fibrillation."（ref 10 REVEAL-AFMR, ref 47 Wagner/Michigan）
  - Section 1 でも同じレンジ: "AFMR and atrial fibrillation have a closely intertwined relationship, with 60% to 80% of AFMR being associated with atrial fibrillation."
  - → **分母・分子（分数）は原文に無く、レンジ%のみ。95%CI・P値の記載なし。**
- **AFMR 特異的エビデンスは限定的であることを自認**: "Although direct evidence evaluating surgical ablation specifically in AFMR remains limited, the strong association between atrial fibrillation burden and severity of MR supports a combined operative strategy."
- 機序的正当化: "Restoration of sinus rhythm through catheter ablation has been shown to reduce MR severity, suggesting that successful surgical ablation might increase the durability of mitral valve repair in these patients."（ref 1 = Gertz ZM, et al. J Am Coll Cardiol. 2011;58(14):1474-1481）
- ★**AFMR での外科アブレーションは変性MRより成績が悪い**（本レビューの核心的知見）:
  - "When compared with surgical ablation in patients with degenerative mitral valve disease, surgical ablation in those with AFMR is less successful."（ref 76 = Song K, et al. J Cardiothorac Surg. 2024;19(1):433）
  - "By 5 years postsurgery, 40% to 60% of patients will experience recurrence of atrial fibrillation."（ref 72 = Bakir NH, et al. JTCVS 2025;170(1):177-186.e3 / ref 89 = Kim K, et al. Korean Circ J. 2023;53(8):550-562）
  - → **%のみ、分数・95%CI・P値の記載なし。**
  - "Therefore, long-term rhythm monitoring is essential."
- 未解決点: "Further studies are necessary to determine whether AFMR patients with successful ablation derive clinical benefits, including greater freedoms from anticoagulation, thromboembolic events, and the need for anticoagulation."（原文どおり。"anticoagulation" が重複する冗長表現のまま）

### Statement 3.5 — lesion set は biatrial maze（CoR IIa, LoE C-expert opinion）★

> "Statement 3.5: For patients undergoing surgical ablation for atrial fibrillation in the setting of AFMR surgery, biatrial maze should be considered. (CoR IIa, LoE C-expert opinion)."

本文の根拠（原文どおり）:
- **術式分類**: "Surgical ablation options can be broadly categorized into pulmonary vein isolation (PVI), a left atrial maze, and biatrial maze (Cox-Maze III/IV)."
- ★**孤立性 PVI の否定**: "Isolated surgical PVI is not recommended for patients with persistent and long-standing persistent atrial fibrillation, regardless of the presence or absence of significant MR."
- ★**biatrial Cox-Maze III/IV を advanced stage の第一選択に**: "In advanced stages of atrial fibrillation, biatrial Cox-Maze III/IV is the preferred surgical ablation approach allowing for more extensive lesion set potentially resulting in >90% sinus rhythm maintenance at 3 years."
  - 根拠 ref 90 = Ad N, et al. *Minimally invasive stand-alone Cox-maze procedure for patients with nonparoxysmal atrial fibrillation.* Ann Thorac Surg. 2013;96(3):792-798 / ref 91 = **2024 ESC guidelines for the management of atrial fibrillation (with EACTS).** Eur Heart J. 2024;45(36):3314-3414
  - → ">90% sinus rhythm maintenance at 3 years" は**二次引用**であり、本コンセンサス自身が算出した数値ではない。**95%CI・P値の記載なし。リズム判定法（モニタリング手段）は本文書中に記載なし。**
- **AFMR 特異的比較研究は存在しない**と明言: "Although no studies directly compare the relative effectiveness of PVI, left atrial maze, and biatrial maze specifically in the setting of AFMR, comparative data are available from cohorts undergoing concomitant surgical ablation for degenerative mitral valve disease."
- biatrial vs PVI（変性MR・**randomized**）: "In randomized patients undergoing degenerative mitral valve repair, biatrial maze procedure was associated with a significantly lower prevalence of postoperative atrial fibrillation compared with PVI."（ref 92 = Blackstone EH, Chang HL, Rajeswaran J, et al. *Biatrial maze procedure versus pulmonary vein isolation for atrial fibrillation during mitral valve surgery: new analytical approaches and end points.* J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg. 2019;157(1):234-243.e9）
  - → **効果量・95%CI・P値は本コンセンサス本文に一切記載なし**（"significantly lower" のみ）。
- biatrial vs left atrial maze: "With respect to left atrial maze versus biatrial maze in degenerative mitral valve disease, multiple studies have demonstrated superior efficacy of biatrial ablation, particularly in patients with nonparoxysmal atrial fibrillation."（ref 93 = Cappabianca G, et al. J Cardiovasc Electrophysiol. 2019;30(10):2150-2163 [meta-analysis, ペースメーカ植込み原因に着目] / ref 94 = Churyla A, et al. Ann Thorac Surg. 2017;103(6):1858-1865）
- ★**left atrial only が劣る3条件**（原文どおり）: "Left atrial ablation only may closely approximate the efficacy of biatrial lesion sets in selected surgical cohorts, and left atrial only ablation is less effective in patients older than age 75 years, in those with a left atrial diameter >5 cm, and in individuals with atrial fibrillation duration exceeding 5 years."（ref 95 = Ad N, Holmes SD, Lamont D, Shuman DJ. Ann Thorac Surg. 2017;103(1):58-65）
- ★**AFMR 集団はまさにこの3条件に該当するという論理**（本コンセンサス独自の推論）:
  - "In the real-world observational study for investigating the prevalence and therapeutic options for atrial functional mitral regurgitation cohort, the mean age of AFMR patients was 77 years, and the average left atrial diameter was 5.1 cm, findings that argue against a left-sided-only ablation strategy for most patients with AFMR."（ref 10 = REVEAL-AFMR）
  - → **mean 77歳・LA径平均 5.1 cm。SD/IQRの記載なし。**
- ★**右房 lesion set の追加根拠**: "Furthermore, inclusion of right atrial lesion sets is recommended in the presence of right atrial dilation or TR, both of which are common findings in AFMR."（ref 96 = McCarthy PM. *The maze IV operation is not always the best choice: matching the procedure to the patient.* JTCVS Tech. 2023;17:79-83）
- ★**biatrial のデメリットも明記**: "Although biatrial ablation is generally the most appropriate strategy in AFMR, it is associated with longer cardiopulmonary bypass time and an increased risk of conduction disturbances requiring permanent pacemaker implantation. These potential downsides should also be considered when selecting the optimal surgical ablation approach for patients with AFMR."（ref 94）
  - → **CPB時間の具体値・PPI率の具体値は Statement 3.5 中には記載なし**（PPI率は 3.3 と 3.8 に別数値あり、下記）。

### Statement 3.6 — LAA exclusion（CoR I, LoE B-nonrandomized）

> "Statement 3.6: In patients with AFMR and atrial fibrillation undergoing cardiac surgery, left atrial appendage exclusion is recommended. (CoR I, LoE B-nonrandomized)."

- "The left atrial appendage (LAA) is the origin of thrombus in more than 90% of AF-related strokes, making it a strategic target for embolic prevention in patients with atrial fibrillation."（ref 97 = LAAOS III 本体, Whitlock RP, et al. N Engl J Med. 2021;384(22):2081-2091）
- AFMR 特異性は無い旨を明記: "Although not specific to AFMR, these findings are likely applicable to patients with AFMR."
- **メタ解析①（22研究）**: "Meta-analysis of 22 studies, reported that surgical LAA exclusion was associated with a marked reduction in long-term stroke or thromboembolism (40%-50% relative reduction) and a statistically significant reduction in overall mortality over follow-up."（ref 98 = Friedman DJ, et al. JAMA. 2018;319(4):365-374）
  - → **40%-50% relative reduction はレンジ表記のみ。95%CI・P値の記載なし。死亡率低下の効果量も記載なし。**
- **メタ解析②（280,585例）**: 脳卒中/塞栓イベント "significant reduction (**hazard ratio, 0.64; 95% CI, 0.53-0.77**)"、中・遠隔期死亡 "significant reduction (**hazard ratio, 0.72; 95% CI, 0.67-0.78**)"（ref 99 = Martín Gutiérrez E, et al. Eur J Cardiothorac Surg. 2020;57(2):252-262）
  - → **HRとCIは原文どおり。P値の記載なし（"significant" のみ）。**
- **LAAOS III（最強エビデンス）**:
  - "enrolled 4811 patients with atrial fibrillation and CHA2DS2-VASc ≥2"
  - "At a median 3.8-year follow-up, stroke or systemic embolism occurred in **4.8%** of patients with LAA exclusion with anticoagulation versus **7.0%** in patients receiving anticoagulation therapy only, yielding an absolute risk reduction of **2.2%** and a 33% relative reduction (**hazard ratio, 0.67; 95% CI, 0.53-0.85**)."
  - → **P値の記載なし。分数（イベント数/N）の記載なし＝原文は%のみ。**
  - "Notably, **77%** of participants remained on anticoagulation therapy, confirming an incremental protective effect beyond pharmacotherapy while the same protective effect was also observed in patients not taking long-term anticoagulation therapy."
  - "Rates of major bleeding, heart failure hospitalization, and perioperative complications were not significantly different between the groups."（ref 100 = Connolly SJ, et al. Circulation. 2023;148(17):1298-1304）
  - → LAAOS III 本体の median follow-up は本文中 "median 3.8-year"。IQR の記載なし。

### Statement 3.7 — LAA 閉鎖の「手技の確実性」と術中確認（CoR I, LoE A）★手技論として重要

> "Statement 3.7: Surgical management of the LAA is recommended with a reliable technique and verified at the time of operation to be complete. (CoR I, LoE A)."

- 二次引用の Class I: "Following the LAAOS III trial, current guidelines support Class I designation for exclusion of the LAA for patients with atrial fibrillation at the time of cardiac surgery."（ref 101 = **2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS AF guideline**, Joglar JA, et al. Circulation. 2024;149(1):e1-e156）
  - → この "Class I" は**二次引用**（2023 ACC/AHA ガイドラインの Class I）。本コンセンサス自身の CoR は上記 "CoR I, LoE A"。
- LAAOS III における TEE 確認の実際: "Notably, the LAAOS III trial required transesophageal echocardiogram confirmation of complete appendage exclusion for patients undergoing suture ligation of the appendage without excision when done through a minithoracotomy. Intraoperative transesophageal echocardiogram was recommended, but not mandated, to verify complete exclusion of the LAA. Most patients continued to receive ongoing antithrombotic therapy."
- ★**手技別の不完全閉鎖リスク**: "Historically, incomplete appendage closure was identified more frequently in patients undergoing suture exclusion or stapler ligation compared with surgical excision with suture closure in patients with follow-up transesophageal echocardiogram after cardiac surgery."（ref 104 = Kanderian AS, Gillinov AM, et al. J Am Coll Cardiol. 2008;52(11):924-929）
- "Incomplete exclusion was associated with a high rate of LAA thrombus, likely increasing the risk of thromboembolic events in this subgroup. In 2 small studies evaluating double-layer suture closure, thromboembolic events occurred in **approximately 25%** of patients with incomplete closure."（ref 105 = Aryana A, et al. Heart Rhythm. 2015;12(7):1431-1437 / ref 106 = Güner A, et al. ISOLATE Registry, J Thromb Thrombolysis. 2021;51(4):1078-1089）
  - → **"approximately 25%" のみ。分数・95%CI・P値の記載なし。**
- "Small residual necks were associated with higher rates of thromboembolic events, whereas no events were observed in the small number of patients with a partial residual stump without a reduced neck, underscoring the importance of complete closure of the appendage in patients with atrial fibrillation."
- ★**クリップ（device-based）の成績**:
  - "In an early multicenter trial examining the effectiveness of the device at the time of median sternotomy, **98%** of patients were found to have effective exclusion of the LAA on either follow-up computed tomography angiogram or transesophageal echocardiogram 3 months after the procedure."（ref 107 = Ailawadi G, Gerdisch MW, et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg. 2011;142(5):1002-1009）
  - "Subsequent evolution of the device led to a thoracoscopic application.   - → **%のみ、分数・95%CI・P値の記載なし。**
- ★**完全閉鎖の定義（術中判定基準）**: "Complete exclusion is verified with transesophageal echocardiogram demonstrating the lack of color Doppler flow into the LAA and collapsed LAA cavity."（ref 101）

### Statement 3.8 — 同時三尖弁形成（CoR IIa, LoE C-expert opinion）

> "Statement 3.8: When repairing AFMR in patients with atrial fibrillation, concomitant tricuspid valve repair should be considered for moderate or greater TR. (CoR IIa, LoE C-expert opinion)."

- "In a large population-based cohort, nearly one-third of patients with atrial fibrillation developed moderate or greater TR over time, and the presence of TR was associated with significantly worse survival."（ref 111 = Patlolla SH, Schaff HV, et al. J Am Coll Cardiol. 2022;80(24):2289-2298）→ **"nearly one-third" のみ。効果量・CI・P値の記載なし。**
- ★**外科的AFアブレーションを行っても遠隔期TRは進行する**（本レビューにとって重要）:
  - "after mitral valve repair for degenerative mitral valve disease, preoperative atrial fibrillation was independently associated with the development of late TR, even in patients with less-than-moderate TR at the time of surgery, **despite concomitant surgical ablation**."（ref 112 = McCarthy PM, Szlapka M, Kruse J, et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg. 2021;161(6):2030-2040.e3）
  - "After surgery for ischemic secondary MR, preoperative atrial fibrillation, **even when treated with surgical atrial fibrillation ablation**, was likewise strongly associated with late moderate or greater TR. ## 7. 術後リズム／抗凝固管理（Section 5）★

### Statement 5.1 — 3か月の予防的抗不整脈薬（CoR IIa, LoE B-nonrandomized）
- 病態的根拠: "Early postoperative atrial fibrillation is frequent after mitral surgery with concomitant surgical ablation for AFMR. - ★**唯一のRCT根拠（外科領域）**: "The strongest evidence in the surgical arena comes from randomized trial, including **90** patients undergoing surgical atrial fibrillation ablation, where a **3-month course of prophylactic amiodarone significantly reduced the composite of atrial arrhythmia recurrence, need for cardioversion or repeat ablation, or crossover to antiarrhythmic drug therapy (19% vs 52%; P = .001)**."（ref 123 = Ad N, Holmes SD, Shuman DJ, Pritchard G, Miller CE. *Amiodarone after surgical ablation for atrial fibrillation: is it really necessary?   - → **P = .001 は原文どおり。95%CI の記載なし（"95%CI記載なし"）。分数の記載なし＝原文は%のみ。**
  - 限界も明記: "However, **approximately 20%** of trial participants underwent isolated maze procedures, and **only half** underwent concomitant mitral surgery. Patients with AFMR who undergo mitral valve intervention and ablation often have more extensive atrial disease, greater postoperative inflammatory burden, and abrupt changes in atrial loading after correction of MR. Thus, although informative, these data require cautious extrapolation to the AFMR population."
- **カテーテルアブレーション領域のメタ解析**: "A meta-analysis of **12** randomized trials including **3625** patients showed that short-term antiarrhythmic drug therapy (>2 months) after catheter ablation significantly reduced early atrial fibrillation recurrence compared with no antiarrhythmic drug, although it did not influence late recurrence beyond the blanking period."（ref 124 = Chen G, et al. - 実務指針: "a short, protocolized 3-month course of prophylactic antiarrhythmic drug (**most commonly amiodarone**, with alternatives selected based on comorbidities and QT interval) is a reasonable strategy... Careful monitoring for drug toxicity and **intentional discontinuation in patients who remain in sinus rhythm** are key elements of this approach."

- 弁関連の根拠: "Consideration of at least 3 months of systemic anticoagulation after surgical mitral repair or after implantation of a mitral bioprosthesis is recommended, even in patients without another indication for oral anticoagulation, due to thrombotic risk associated with suture lines, prosthetic material, and incomplete endothelialization."（ref 21, 125, 126）

- ★**カテーテルアブレーション後の抗凝固中止の新エビデンス（外科ではない点を明記）**: "The optimal anticoagulation for higher risk patients post-catheter ablation for atrial fibrillation (**OCEAN**) and anticoagulation one year after ablation of atrial fibrillation in patients with atrial fibrillation (**ALONE-AF**) trials demonstrated the safety of **aspirin monotherapy, without additional anticoagulation, in patients who were at least 1 year removed from successful catheter-based ablation**.   - ref 127 = Verma A, Birnie DH, Jiang C, et al. N Engl J Med. 2026;394:323-332（OCEAN）
  - ref 128 = Kim D, Shim J, Choi EK, et al. ALONE-AF randomized clinical trial. JAMA. 2025;334(14):1246-1254

### Statement 5.3 — LAA閉鎖後の抗凝固中止（CoR IIa, LoE C-limited data）

## 11. 本レビュー（Maze／外科的AFアプローチ動向）にとっての含意

**原著の主張（本文書が明示的に述べていること）**
1. **AFMR 外科では同時 AF アブレーションが CoR I（LoE B-nonrandomized）**。AFMR 特異的な直接エビデンスは無いにもかかわらず Class I を与えている（変性MRからの外挿＋病態論理による）。
2. **lesion set は biatrial maze（Cox-Maze III/IV）を "should be considered"（CoR IIa, LoE C-expert opinion）**。孤立性 surgical PVI は persistent/long-standing persistent AF では**明確に否定**（"is not recommended"）。左房のみの ablation は「75歳超・LA径>5cm・AF罹病5年超で劣る」ため、平均77歳・LA径5.1cm の AFMR 集団には不適合、という論法。
3. **右房 lesion 追加の理由づけが AFMR 特異的**: 右房拡大や TR が高頻度 → 右房 lesion を含めるべき。
4. **biatrial のコストも明記**: CPB 時間延長、伝導障害／PPI 増加。MVP+TVP+biatrial maze で PPI **約11-15%**。
5. **AFMR での surgical ablation は変性MRより成功率が低く、5年で 40-60% が AF 再発**する。長期リズムモニタリングが必須。
6. **LAA は CoR I 2本立て**: 「閉鎖すること」(3.6) と「確実な手技で術中に完全性を確認すること」(3.7, LoE A)。クリップの完全閉鎖率 ∼95-98% vs 縫合／ステープル劣位、不完全閉鎖で塞栓イベント約25%。不完全なら**その場で suture reinforcement**（クリップの付け直しは LAA 損傷リスク）。
7. **弁形成が第一選択**（annuloplasty、anterior leaflet 面積基準で1-2サイズ down-size、band/ring の別は耐久性に影響なし）。10年 freedom from MR ≥3+ は 80-89%。
8. **弁介入の前にまず rhythm control を試みる**（CoR IIa, LoE B-NR）。ただし残存 AFMR がある場合に介入を遅らせてはならない。
9. **術後3か月のアミオダロン予防投与（CoR IIa）と最低3か月の抗凝固継続（CoR IIa）**。LAA閉鎖後の抗凝固中止は heart team 判断（CoR IIa, LoE C-LD）。
10. **AF アブレーションを行っても遠隔期 TR は進行する**（変性MR・虚血性MR のいずれの外科データでも "despite/even when treated with surgical ablation" と明記）→ 中等度以上 TR には同時 TVP を。

**読み手（心臓外科医）としての推論（原著の主張ではない）**
1. 3.4 が CoR I／LoE B-NR、3.5 が CoR IIa／LoE C-EO という格差は、「**アブレーションをやるか否か**」は確立、「**どこまで焼くか（lesion set）**」は依然としてエキスパートオピニオン、という現状を正確に反映している。本レビューで lesion set 論争を扱う際、AFMR 領域では RCT が皆無であることを前提に据えるべき。
2. 「AFMR 群の外科成績が VFMR より良いのは concomitant maze のおかげ」という因果解釈は、原著自身が "may be attributed to" と留保した仮説である。交絡（LV機能保持）と分離されていない。
3. AFMR は高齢・巨大左房という「Maze が最も効きにくい基質」でありながら biatrial を推奨する、という緊張関係がある。PPI 11-15% を許容できるか、そのトレードオフの定量的評価は本文書に無い。
4. リズム転帰の判定基準（blanking、>30秒、AT/AFL、AAD 下の判定、モニタ手段）が一切定義されていないため、本文書の「40-60% 再発」「70% at 1 year」「>90% at 3 years」の各数値は**互いに比較不能**である。統合レビューでこれらを並べる際は判定法の非統一を明示すべき。

## 12. 限界

**【著者記載】**
- AFMR 特異的エビデンスの欠如: "Although direct evidence evaluating surgical ablation specifically in AFMR remains limited..."（3.4）／"no studies directly compare the relative effectiveness of PVI, left atrial maze, and biatrial maze specifically in the setting of AFMR"（3.5）／"The effect of GDMT specifically in patients with AFMR is not well studied"（2.2）／"Data are limited for transcatheter treatment of AFMR"（1.3）
- 変性MRデータからの外挿であることの自認（3.4, 3.5, 5.1）: "these data require cautious extrapolation to the AFMR population."（5.1）
- REVEAL-AFMR 外科例のサンプルサイズの小ささ: "Although the sample size was small, there was no statistically significant difference in the survival between those undergoing repair and replacement"
- 研究優先課題（Section 6）: 統一 AFMR 分類の確立、前向きレジストリ（画像フェノタイプ＋リズム転帰の対応づけ）、annuloplasty を超える adjunctive repair 手技の検証、**外科 vs カテーテル治療の RCT**（エンドポイント: repair durability, rhythm control durability, structural remodeling）、左房逆リモデリングの縦断評価。
- 対象外領域の明示（severe atrial functional TR without significant MR）。

**【読み手として】**
- Delphi の各ステートメント別 agreement % が非開示（採択閾値 ≥75% のみ提示）。反対意見の分布が追えない。
- 文献検索が PubMed 単独・検索式非開示・件数非開示で、"systematic literature review" と称するには PRISMA 準拠の記述が欠けている。
- **Figure 2 凡例の LAVI >34 mL/m² と本文の LA volume index >40 mL/m² が不一致**。どちらを採用すべきか本文書内で解決されていない。
- AtriCure（LAA クリップ・アブレーションデバイス）との関係を持つ執筆者が複数おり、クリップ優位（∼95-98%）と LAA CoR I／LoE A の推奨には COI の観点が必要。
- LoE A が付与された 2 件（1.2 imaging、3.7 LAA 手技の完全性確認）のうち、3.7 の「術中 TEE での完全性確認」自体を RCT で検証した研究は本文中に示されていない（LAAOS III でも TEE は "recommended, but not mandated"）。LoE A の根拠が読み取りにくい。
- 数値の多くが「約」「レンジ」表記で、95%CI・P値がほぼ付されていない（明示があるのは ref 99 の HR 2 本、LAAOS III の HR 0.67、Ad RCT の P = .001 のみ）。

## 13. 注意 — この文書が触れていない論点（本レビューで別文献を要する箇所）

1. **胸腔鏡下（thoracoscopic / totally endoscopic）AF 手術・ハイブリッドアブレーション**: LAA クリップの胸腔鏡応用（"Subsequent evolution of the device led to a thoracoscopic application."）に触れる以外、**stand-alone thoracoscopic ablation・convergent/hybrid procedure に関する推奨は一切ない**。
2. **PFA（pulsed field ablation）**: 本文書中に PFA の言及は**皆無**（エネルギー源として cryo / radiofrequency の別も論じられていない）。AFMR 手術における energy source の推奨なし。
3. **Cox-Maze IV の具体的 lesion set の記述**（box lesion、mitral isthmus/coronary sinus line、cavotricuspid isthmus、Marshall靭帯 等）が**一切ない**。"biatrial maze (Cox-Maze III/IV)" というラベルのみ。
4. **リズム転帰の操作的定義**（blanking period の長さ、>30秒閾値、AT/AFL の扱い、AAD off/on の判定）と**モニタリングプロトコル**（Holter/イベントレコーダ/ILR の種類・頻度・実施率）の指定がない。
5. **AF 病型別（paroxysmal / persistent / long-standing persistent）の AFMR 患者内訳**の数値がない（3.5 で "advanced stages of atrial fibrillation" と定性的に述べるのみ）。
6. **左房縮小術（LA reduction/plication）**の是非に関する記載がない（AFMR は巨大左房が本態であるにもかかわらず）。
7. **severe atrial functional TR without significant MR** の管理（著者が明示的に対象外と宣言）。
8. **カテーテルアブレーション後に AFMR が残存した場合の外科的サルベージのタイミング**についての具体的閾値がない（"intervention should not be delayed" という定性的記述のみ）。
9. **費用・医療経済、術者/施設ボリューム閾値**に関する記載がない。
