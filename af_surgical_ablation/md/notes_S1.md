# 精読ノート S1 — ガイドライン・コンセンサス（土台）

> セッションA / 対象 6編 / 作成 2026-07-23
> 本ノートは統合レビュー執筆（セッションF）で**これだけを読めば足りる**ことを目指す。

## このセクションの結論（3-5行）

1. **同じ根拠試験を使いながら、同時外科的アブレーションの推奨クラスは学会間で4段階に割れている。** 僧帽弁併施は STS 2023 が **Class I / LOE A**、ESC/EACTS 2024 も **I / A**（2020年版 IIa/A からの格上げ）だが、ACC/AHA 2023 は心臓手術全般で **Class 2a / LOE B-R** にとどまり、EHRA/HRS/APHRS/LAHRS 2024 は Class 体系を使わず **"Advice TO DO"（META）**、AATS 2026（AFMR限定）は **CoR I / LoE B-nonrandomized**。**非僧帽弁ではさらに開く — STS 2023 は "any first-time nonemergent concomitant nonmitral operation" に I / B-NR、ESC/EACTS 2024 は IIa / B**。根拠はいずれも Cochrane 22 RCT（洞調律 RR 2.04, 95%CI 1.63–2.55／PPM RR 1.69, 95%CI 1.12–2.54）と CTSN RCT（1年 AF-free 63% vs 29%）というほぼ同一のセットである。
2. **LAAOS III だけが全文書を一斉に動かした。** STS 2023 は **IIa/C → I/A**（2段階）、ESC/EACTS 2024 は **IIb/C → I/B**、ACC/AHA 2023 は **Class 1 / LOE A**（ただし "in addition to continued anticoagulation"、抗凝固なしでの利益は **2b/A = uncertain**）、EHRA/HRS 2024 は **Advice TO DO（RAND）**、AATS 2026 は **CoR I / LoE B-NR**。効果量の中核は一致（stroke or systemic embolism **4.8% vs 7.0%、HR 0.67, 95%CI 0.53–0.85, P=.001**）だが、**症例数が 4770（STS・ACC/AHA）と 4811（ESC/EACTS・AATS）に割れ、追跡も "mean 3.8 years"（STS・ESC・EHRA・ACC/AHA）と "median 3.8-year"（AATS）で食い違う → 要確認**。
3. **lesion set に踏み込むのは EHRA/HRS 2024 と AATS 2026 の2文書だけ。** EHRA/HRS は「**biatrial Cox maze または最低でも PVI ＋ 左房後壁隔離**」を Advice TO DO（RAND）とし、根拠に **後壁全体を隔離できなかった群の5年 AF-free off AADs 33% vs 隔離群 66%（P = 0.017）**、僧帽弁例では後壁不完全隔離が**唯一の独立予測因子で AF再発 6.7倍**を挙げる。AATS 2026 は AFMR で **biatrial maze を CoR IIa / LoE C-expert opinion**（＝「やるか否か」は I なのに「どこまで焼くか」は専門家意見）。**ESC/EACTS 2024 と ACC/AHA 2023 は lesion set に一切推奨を与えていない**（ESC は "lesion set" が参考文献タイトル内のみ、ACC/AHA は "Cox-maze" が引用文献名にしか登場しない）。STS 2023 の唯一の lesion set 系推奨は **「LA ≥4.5 cm または中等度超MRに対する PVI 単独は Class III / LOE C」**。
4. **POAF では posterior pericardiotomy だけが3文書で一致し、抗凝固では STS 2026 が一人だけ格下げした。** Posterior pericardiotomy は ESC/EACTS **IIa/B（OR 0.44, 95%CI 0.27–0.70, P=.0005）**、ACC/AHA **2a/B-R（PALACS 17% vs 32%, P=0.0007, OR 0.44）**、STS 2026 **IIa/B-R（メタ解析 22研究4300例で 11.7% vs 23.7%, OR 0.49, 95%CI 0.38–0.61）** と揃う。一方 POAF後の長期抗凝固は ACC/AHA **2a**・ESC/EACTS **IIa** に対し **STS 2026 は IIb** — 根拠は STS ACSD 166,747例で ischemic stroke **aOR 0.87 (95%CI 0.65–1.16, P=.35)** と減らず、大出血 **aOR 4.30 (95%CI 3.69–5.03, P<.001)**、30日死亡 **aOR 1.20 (95%CI 1.02–1.40, P=.024)** と害だけが出たこと。
5. **6文書に共通する巨大な空白は PFA・AFMR・リズム判定プロトコルの3つ。** PFA は STS 2023 で言及ゼロ、ACC/AHA 2023 で 0件、ESC/EACTS 2024 はカテーテル文脈で1箇所（607例RCTで非劣性）のみ・外科PFAはゼロ、EHRA/HRS 2024 は hybrid の endocardial 側について将来形の1文のみ、STS 2026 も記載ゼロ（系統的検索は2024年12月31日までだが、原文は検索期間後の2025年文献も本文に採用しているため、**検索期間だけでは PFA 不記載を説明できない**）。AFMR は ESC/EACTS・EHRA/HRS で完全非収載（ESC/EACTS は "AFMR"/"atrial functional mitral"/"secondary mitral" が0ヒットで "mitral regurgitation" も DOAC 適応の文脈に1箇所のみ、EHRA/HRS は "regurgitation" が文書全体で0ヒット）、ACC/AHA は「洞調律維持の便益の根拠」として53例・70%の観察研究を挙げるのみ、実質的に扱うのは AATS 2026 と STS 2023（概念記述のみ・数値なし）の2文書。**そして外科的アブレーション後の blanking period・>30秒閾値・AT/AFL の扱いを定義した文書は6編中ゼロ**（STS 2023 が「1年時 AF free かつ AAD free」を成功と定義し "at least periodic 24-hour Holter" を求めるのが最も具体的、EHRA/HRS の **8週 blanking** はカテーテル文脈だが "future clinical trial design" と述べ対象を外科に限定していない）。

- **リズム判定** ★（6編中で最も具体的）:
  - 成功の定義（本文 Section 7、逐語）: "After surgical ablation, it is suggested that patients be longitudinally monitored for a minimum of 1 year by the surgeon of the multidisciplinary heart team. **The measure of success in surgical ablation is freedom from atrial fibrillation and antiarrhythmic drugs at 1 year.**"

- **根拠として引用された試験・数値（原文にある数値のみ）**:
  - **CTSN RCT（ref 46）**: "260 patients with persistent or long-standing persistent atrial fibrillation underwent pulmonary vein isolation, a biatrial maze procedure, or no ablation during concomitant mitral valve surgery. Ablation resulted in significant higher freedom from atrial fibrillation at 12 months (**63% vs 29%**), a higher risk of permanent pacemaker implantation, and lower mortality (**6.8% vs 8.7%**), although this did not reach statistical significance." — **95%CI・P値・分数の記載なし**。
  - **ポーランド全国レジストリ（ref 71）**: 僧帽弁手術3568例、median follow-up 5年、全死亡 **HR 0.82 (95%CI 0.7-0.96)**（P値記載なし）。
  - **Kim ら リウマチ性MV（ref 69）**: "monitoring 1229 patients for more than 5 years"。効果量の記載なし。
  - **RCT16件のメタ解析（ref 44）**: isolated AVR と CABG の両サブグループで1年時洞調律有病率がSA群で高い。"There were no significant differences between the ablation and no-ablation groups regarding all-cause mortality, pacemaker implantation, and neurologic events."（数値なし）。
  - **ネットワークメタ解析 2031例（ref 100）**: "suggested greater effectiveness with more extensive ablation, including open left atrial or biatrial maze vs PVI alone. However, this study showed no further improvement in conversion to sinus rhythm with a biatrial maze. The left atrial maze was also not associated with increased operative morbidity or mortality compared with PVI alone. **The network meta-analysis did suggest increased mortality with the addition of the right atrial maze**; importantly, the individuals included in the biatrial maze group underwent more complex procedures and were higher-risk patients."（数値なし）。★**右房病変の追加を死亡と結びつけた唯一の記述**。
  - **AKI（ref 79・単施設）**: **OR 1.89 (95%CI 1.12-3.18, P = .017)**、CPB時間とは独立。"there was not an increased risk of permanent dialysis, nor did the higher incidence of acute kidney injury impact the survival benefit seen with surgical ablation."

  - ★**変換不良と関連する因子（逐語・lesion set 論として最重要）**: "Variables that are consistently found to be associated with lower rates of conversion to sinus rhythm after surgical ablation include **large left atrium, advanced age, cumulative atrial fibrillation burden, a less extensive ablation procedure, in particular, omission or incompleteness of the left posterior wall isolation, and the coronary sinus lesion.**"
  - 早期POAF減少: "Several randomized clinical trials of mitral patients have established surgical ablation as an effective intervention to reduce the prevalence of early postoperative atrial fibrillation by **more than 50%**."（refs 40-43、効果量なし）。
  - **非僧帽弁（closed atrial）**: Damiano 2003 の Cox maze III + CABG で "**a 98% sinus rhythm rate at 5 years**"（ref 90）。CABG±PVI の小規模RCT 35例（ref 94）で "At 18 months, **89%** of patients in the PVI group were atrial fibrillation-free vs **47%** in the coronary artery bypass grafting-only group."。**★lesion set による差**: "rhythm end point recovery seems to approximate **50% to 80% with PVI alone** compared with **>90% with full open atrial biatrial maze procedures**."（refs 90, 98, 99）。台湾全国レジストリ（ref 67、約7000例）で5年の全死亡・脳卒中・血栓塞栓の有意な減少（数値なし）。Medicare 100,000例（Mehaffey JTCVS 2024, ref 101）で "reduced 3-year mortality and a significant reduction in stroke ... when compared with **left atrial appendage occlusion alone**"（★比較対照がLAAO単独、数値なし）。

- **stand-alone SA・胸腔鏡・ハイブリッド（本文）**:
  - Cox maze IV ≒ III: "the efficacy of bipolar radiofrequency plus cryoablation (Cox maze IV) to complete the lesion set was equivalent to the Cox maze III technique for stand-alone surgical ablation, **if both were applied meticulously**."（refs 97, 106）
  - 胸腔鏡下 off-pump RF PVI + LAA切除: "For the **60% to 80%** of patients who attain the rhythm end point, antiarrhythmic and anticoagulant agents are eventually discontinued."（refs 107-113）

  - **GPアブレーションの否定**: "ganglionic ablation has been minimally efficacious and is **no longer considered as an additive application** to surgical ablation. In a randomized clinical trial, additional ganglionic ablation did not result in greater procedural success but **significantly increased major morbidity, including bleeding, sinus node dysfunction, and need for permanent pacemaker implantation**."（refs 58, 125, 126）
  - **lesion set の原則**: "Most studies suggest the relative superiority of extended left atrial and biatrial lesion sets over PVI ... **Surgeons should be aware that incomplete lesion sets can be proarrhythmic and have been implicated in the induction of atypical macro-reentrant atrial flutter.**"（refs 96, 127, 128）

  - ★★**総括的な立場（本レビューにとって決定的）**: "Considering the totality of the evidence, **more complete lesion sets, particularly a biatrial Cox maze III/IV procedure, applied through a minimally invasive approach, may provide the greatest benefit overall.**"／"An increasing number of stand-alone surgical ablation studies using cryoablation and a minimally invasive thoracoscopic or robotic-assisted approach have recently shown improved outcomes ... with **>90% of patients being free from atrial fibrillation at 1 year and 80% at 5 years of follow up**. ... **These were not always documented by continuous electrocardiographic monitoring to support the rhythm end points.**"（refs 26, 138-142）／"the field awaits more homogeneous or randomized evidence on hybrid or epicardial ablation procedures that adhere to the concept of the Cox maze lesion set. **Epicardial ablation with atypical lesions remains exploratory until more robust evidence becomes available.**"
  - 左房縮小術: "Because left atrial size is a risk factor for ablation failure, atrial reduction procedures may have benefit, although this **remains controversial and without strong evidence**."（refs 141-144）→ **S6 と接続する記述。推奨なし。**

Complication rates attributed to surgery were just **3.2%**."／STS National Database（ref 146 = Ad N, JTCVS 2012）= "an operative mortality rate of **0.74%**. The complication rate was considerably higher at **16.43%**, although major morbidities, such as stroke (**0.72%**), renal failure (**2.45%**), and bleeding (**0.99%**), were low. Pacemakers were implanted in **1.03%**."（すべて%のみ、分数・CIなし）。

- **術式分類と用語（12.1）**:
  - 3群分類（逐語）: "(i) a full, biatrial Cox maze procedure; (ii) PVI or posterior LA isolation alone, or (iii) PVI combined with an extended LA lesion set. **All surgical AF ablation approaches are combined with LAA exclusion.**"
  - ★**"Cox maze procedure" の用語の厳格化（逐語）**: "At present, it is recommended that the term 'Cox maze procedure' is appropriately used **only to refer to a biatrial lesion set including specific transmural lesions that extend between non-conductive tissues (valve annulus or vena cava or another lesion; Figure 14)**."（根拠: McGilvray/Damiano JTCVS Tech 2023）
  - "The best late rhythm outcomes have been shown with the **full biatrial Cox maze procedure**, while a certain subgroup of patients, such as those with paroxysmal AF, have reasonable results with more limited lesion sets."
  - **ABLATE trial**（bipolar RF、FDA承認の根拠、prospective multicenter non-RCT、非発作性AFで CABG および/または弁手術＋Cox maze IV）: "**a 76% freedom from AF recurrence off AADs at 6 months with a major peri-operative adverse event rate of 9%**."（95%CI・N数の記載なし）
- **エネルギー源とデバイス（12.1.1–12.1.2）— transmurality の実データ ★**:
  - 対象は RF と cryo のみ。"**While there have not been any RCTs to compare the efficacy of one ablation technology with another**, these technologies have had proven clinical efficiency over the last two decades."
  - **Unipolar RF**: "The unipolar devices **do not provide surgeons with transmurality indicators**." / "Unlike bipolar RF devices, unipolar ones have **failed to consistently create transmural lesions** and have a risk of forming endocardial char or thrombus." / **FDA承認なし**（唯一の承認 unipolar デバイスは hybrid 用の **EPi-Sense（AtriCure）**）。
  - **Bipolar clamp（Isolator Synergy, AtriCure）**: 併施心臓手術でのAF治療に**FDA承認された唯一の**デバイス。慢性動物実験では single application で全病変 transmural。しかし**ヒト ex-vivo 心臓摘出モデルでは "a single application resulted in only 65% of lesions being transmural throughout their entire length."** 非transmural化の要因は "**increased thickness of atrial tissue and the presence of epicardial fat**"。**"Application of two successive ablations without unclamping resulted in 100% lesion transmurality."**（95%CI・P値なし）
  - **irrigated bipolar RF clamp（Cardioblate BP2/LP, Medtronic）**: 同モデルで "a double application without unclamping results in significantly increased rate of lesion transmurality compared with single application (**92 vs. 74%**)."（P値・95%CIなし）
  - **Isolator Synergy EnCompass Clamp（AtriCure）**: in-vivo beating heart モデルで "reliable transmurality and isolation of the entire posterior LA wall and all four PVs with a **single application**"。ただし "Further clinical trials will be needed to test its clinical performance."
  - ★**臨床上の但し書き（対象は Isolator Synergy bipolar clamp。直前の EnCompass Clamp ではない）**: "In a chronic animal study using this device with a single application, all lesions produced were transmural. However, **in clinical experience, multiple applications are needed to achieve exit block.**"（原文ではこの2文は Isolator Synergy clamp の段落冒頭にあり、EnCompass Clamp の記述はその数文後の別段落）
  - **Directional（non-clamp）デバイス**: 焼灼時間 **10–40秒/回**、"with the **highest risk of ablation gaps at the end of the devices**; thus, continuous lesions should be overlapped"。Isolator pen / Coolrail は "creation of transmural lesions has been **inferior to bipolar RF clamps**"。**Coolrail は AEF（atrio-esophageal fistula）の症例報告あり**（"should be used with caution"。対策 "Rinsing the pericardium with saline may be used to prevent AEF"）。
  - **Suction-assisted**: Cobra Fusion 150（uni+bipolar、吸引 **−500 mmHg** を1–2分維持）→ 急性ブタ拍動心で "**68% rate of lesion transmurality**"。EPi-Sense（3 cm、suction、irrigated unipolar RF）は動物実験で "lesion transmurality … has been variable in multiple animal studies from **15 to 100%**"。
  - **Cryo**: "cryothermy creates homogenous scars in a non-directional pattern"／弁組織周囲に理想的（"preserves fibrous tissue and the extracellular matrix"）。欠点は "the relatively longer time to create a lesion (**2–3 min**)" と拍動心での heat sink → ★**"cryoablation should not be used to create epicardial lesions off cardiopulmonary bypass."** 均一連続病変には **−30°C 未満**の到達が必須。デバイス = argon の Cardioblate CryoFlex（Medtronic、最低 **−160°C**）、nitrous oxide の cryoFORM / cryoICE（AtriCure、プローブ最低 **−50 to −70°C**）。cryoICE 慢性ヒツジ心内膜側で transmurality **98%**、CryoFlex 慢性イヌで LAA・PV 病変 **93%**、Cox maze 線状病変 **84%**。**"There have been no surgical cryoablation devices that have yet received an FDA indication for the treatment of AF"**（N2O・argon 両者で臨床試験進行中）。
  - **RF の生物物理**: "Cardiac muscle exposed to temperature of **55°C or higher for more than a few seconds** will show irreversible coagulation necrosis." / 非灌流クランプでは "**Care should be taken to clean the electrodes after every two to three ablations** … because char decreases conductance, which can result in non-transmural lesions. Epicardial fat and muscle thickness can also decrease conductance and limit ablation depth."
- **Lesion set（12.1.3）★**:
  - 前提（逐語）: "**There are insufficient high-quality data on what should be the most important targets during surgical ablation.**"
  - **PVI（12.1.3.1）**: "As with catheter ablation, PVI is a foundational part of all surgical AF ablation procedures. **Although documentation of exit and/or entrance block across PVs is preferred, it is infrequently performed.**"／術中マッピングでは LSPAF＋僧帽弁疾患で LA/RA ともに複雑な activation → "indicating that a **simplified approach with PVI alone may not be adequate** during concomitant surgical ablation."／低侵襲 PVI＋GP＋Marshall靭帯のみの単施設コホート: **5年 single-procedural success 37.8%**（ECG＋transtelephonic monitoring）。／"Retrospective observational data suggest that surgical ablation with PVI alone is **inferior to the biatrial maze procedure** in patients with persistent or long-standing persistent AF."／CTSN（ref 1345）: "One randomized trial on non-paroxysmal AF patients undergoing mitral valve surgery reported **similar rate of freedom from AF with PVI as compared to the biatrial maze procedure. However, the study was underpowered to adequately answer the question of which lesion set was more efficacious.**"
  - ★★**LA posterior wall isolation（12.1.3.2）— 最重要**:
    - "**Isolation of the entire LAPW and all four PVs is the most important part of surgical ablation procedures.**"
    - Cox maze IV の大規模後ろ向き研究（Henn 2015）: **後壁全体を isolate できなかった群は 5年で AF free off AADs が 33%、isolate した群は 66%（P = 0.017）**（95%CI・分数なし＝原文は%のみ）。
    - 僧帽弁手術例ではさらに影響大: "the failure to isolate the entire posterior LA during a Cox maze procedure was **the only independent predictor of procedural failure** and resulted in **6.7-fold increased risk of AF recurrence**."（95%CI・P値なし）
    - 結論: "Due to anticipated improvement in rhythm outcome with LA PWI, **a minimum of PVI plus LA PWI should be performed in patients undergoing surgical AF ablation.**"
  - **RA/LA linear lesions（12.1.3.3）**: CTI（右）と mitral isthmus（左）は macroreentrant AT 予防が目的。胸腔鏡下の単施設連続例で "adjunctive CTI ablation **significantly increased** freedom from atrial tachyarrhythmia recurrence."（効果量・CI・P値なし）。**"The superior-inferior vena cava ablation line anchors the RA isthmus line and thus serves an important role in preventing late RA flutter. Documentation of completeness is beneficial in all deployed linear lesions."**
  - **Ganglionated plexi（12.1.3.4）— 明確な否定**: "The only randomized study examining the efficacy and safety of additional GP ablation in patients undergoing thoracoscopic surgery showed **no incremental benefit in AF recurrence rate and a significantly higher rate of major procedural complications**"（AFACT試験、ref 1008、効果量なし）→ **"Therefore, with the exception of a clinical trial setting, GP ablation should not be performed during surgical AF ablation."**（推奨表には項目化されていない、本文中の記述）
  - **Ligament of Marshall（12.1.3.5）**: "**There are no data from the surgical literature to support the ligament of Marshall as a target for ablation.** However, this structure is usually divided while isolating the left PVs."
  - **Cox maze IV lesion set（Figure 14 図説・逐語）**: "(A) Left atrial lesion set including: (i) left atriotomy, (ii) ablation around the left-sided PVs, (iii) ablation around the right-sided PVs, (iv) **posterior wall box lesion**, (v) line connecting left PV lesion to excluded LAA, (vi) line connecting box lesion to mitral annulus, (vii) **cryoablation to the epicardial ostial region of the coronary sinus (not shown)**. (B) Right atrial lesion set including: (i) right atriotomy extending over crista terminalis, (ii) line from the atriotomy to the superior and inferior vena cava **posterior to the crista terminalis (to avoid injury to the sinoatrial node)**, (iii) line connecting the atriotomy to the tricuspid annulus (**2 o'clock relative to the valve**), and (iv) line connecting the right atriotomy to the right atrial appendage."
- **併施（concomitant）手術（12.2）**:
  - 有効性: **23 RCT のメタ解析**で "AF ablation concomitant to cardiac surgery results in **increased freedom from AF at 12 months**."（**効果量・95%CI・P値いずれも原文に記載なし**）。脳卒中は "Several trials have demonstrated a **reduced incidence of stroke at 5 years**"（数値なし）。
  - ★**長期生存**: "**Improvement of long-term survival after concomitant surgical AF ablation has not been proved by an RCT.**" ただし STS AF database の propensity matching で30日死亡率の低下と関連（数値なし）、"several retrospective and propensity-matched studies as well as large national registries have demonstrated that ... (**particularly mitral valve surgery and CABG**) was associated with improved long-term survival."
  - **isolated CABG**: "In a retrospective propensity score–matched analysis of a nationwide registry, concomitant surgical ablation for AF in patients undergoing **isolated CABG** was shown to **significantly improve long-term survival rates**."（Suwalski 2020 EJCTS、数値なし）
  - 安全性: "Several studies, including RCTs, have demonstrated that **concomitant AF surgery is safe and does not increase operative mortality.**"／AKI は "increased incidence" だが "the associated long-term risks were **offset by the significant survival benefit** derived from the concomitant Cox maze procedure."（数値なし）／**術後AT**: "**Incomplete linear lesions with residual conduction and inappropriate surgical techniques are mainly responsible for postoperative occurrence of predominantly macroreentrant ATs. Intraoperative verification of conduction block, particularly to ensure PVI, may reduce the incidence of AT due to incomplete ablation.**"
  - **ペースメーカ**: "Increased incidence of new permanent pacemaker implantation after the Cox maze procedure has been demonstrated in many studies. **However, in a recent large European registry of patients undergoing valve surgery, surgical AF ablation was not associated with increased need for permanent pacemaker implantation.**" / "**Sinus node dysfunction requiring permanent pacemaker implantation can occur in up to 10% of patients after the Cox maze procedure for non-paroxysmal AF** and may be a result of unmasking preexisting sick sinus syndrome." / 術中要因は "mechanical or thermal injury to the sinus or AV node and interruption of the conduction system arterial supply"。対策 = "**Multidisciplinary collaboration between cardiothoracic surgeons and electrophysiologists, proper training on ablation techniques, and deployment of complete linear lesions**"。
  - **LA open 症例の lesion set（12.2.3）**: "**The biatrial Cox maze procedure is the preferred procedure for surgical AF ablation during open LA procedures** ... However, recognizing that surgical training and experience may vary across centers, the writing group suggests that **biatrial Cox maze procedure or a minimum of PVI plus LA PWI is required**."
  - **closed（non-LA open）症例の lesion set（12.2.4）**: 実施率が低い理由 = "because of the necessity of adding an LA incision"。"**Epicardial PVI alone has been performed more often than a biatrial maze procedure** in AF patients undergoing non-LA open procedures, and **this might have led to biased analyses of the data.**"／"A **dilated LA** has been shown to be associated with worse AF-free and event-free survivals after PVI for patients with paroxysmal AF undergoing non-LA open cardiac surgery."／"Several studies have shown that **biatrial maze procedure is associated with superior rhythm outcome and a lower risk of adverse events and long-term overall mortality compared with LA lesion sets** following surgical AF ablation concomitant to non-mitral valve surgery."（数値なし）／★現実論: "**However, many surgeons are reluctant to increase procedural complexity and risks by performing AF ablation through an atriotomy.** Therefore, selection of PVI combined with PWI or other modified procedure **should be individualized** ... **Further clinical studies are needed to clarify the optimal lesion set in these patients.**"
- **Stand-alone と低侵襲・hybrid（12.3）**:
  - **Stand-alone Cox maze（12.3.1）**

    | 研究 | N | 背景 | 成績（原文どおり） | 安全性 |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | MacGregor 2022（ref 1323、最大シリーズ） | **236 consecutive patients** | 59% prior failed catheter ablation、**median duration of preoperative AF 6.2 years**（IQR記載なし） | "**89 and 77% freedom from recurrent atrial tachyarrhythmias at 5 and 10 years**, respectively, **irrespective of AF type or surgical approach** (median sternotomy vs. minimally invasive)" | "**without 30-day mortality**" |
    | Lapenna 2020（ref 1324） | **59 patients** | stand-alone Cox maze | "**84% of patients remaining in SR at 7 years**" | "without 30-day mortality or late strokes" |
    | Ad 2017（ref 1325） | **133 patients** | minimally invasive stand-alone Cox maze、**78% long-standing persistent AF** | "**a 73% freedom from atrial tachyarrhythmias off AADs at 5 years**" | "with only one late stroke and no associated mortality" |

    - 上記いずれも 95%CI・P値の記載なし。著者らの解釈（逐語）: "**It is important to point out that these procedures were done with cardiopulmonary bypass, which may explain the safety of this procedure.** Unfortunately, **there have been no prospective multicenter trials of the stand-alone treatment of AF with the Cox maze procedure.**"
  - **低侵襲・hybrid の総論（12.3.2）**: 比較の限界 = "**non-uniformity in patient populations and lack of standardized surgical technique, ablation technology and deployed lesion sets**"。hybrid の本質 = "**harmonizes epicardial and endocardial ablation components** to effectively target key drivers of AF, including the PVs and the LAPW."。**single-stage vs dual-stage**: "**Completion of both phases in the same session prolongs procedural time, which may add additional risk in patients with comorbidities.** For dual-staged programs, the epicardial component typically occurs in the cardiac operating room, and the endocardial component is scheduled **~2–4 months later** ... **The impact of different procedural timing on patient outcomes is unknown.**"
  - **胸腔鏡下アプローチ（12.3.2.1）— 手技詳細**: 全身麻酔＋ダブルルーメンチューブ。**右側ポート** = "a camera port ... **fifth intercostal space midaxillary line**, a **5 or 10 mm working port in the sixth or seventh intercostal space anterior axillary line**, and a **5 mm working port in the third intercostal space anterior axillary line**. **The pericardium is opened anterior to the PN**. Blunt dissection is used to open the **transverse and oblique sinuses**. Antral isolation of the right PVs **as a pair** is performed with repetitive applications using a bipolar RF clamp"。**左側** = "The same port incisions are made on the left side but placed **more posteriorly**. **The pericardium is opened posterior to the PN.**"。**重症COPD例の工夫** = "thoracoscopic epicardial isolation of the PVs can be performed **only on the left**, and the right PVs subsequently isolated **from the endocardium** to avoid bilateral sequential lung deflation. An alternative would also be a **convergent procedure using subxiphoid access along with Lariat closure of the LAA.**"。**box作成** = "After PVI documentation, a **roof line (connecting both superior PVs)** and an **inferior line (connecting both inferior PVs)** are made epicardially using directional ablation devices … As an alternative, **one epicardial box lesion including the posterior LA wall and the PVs can be created using only the irrigated bipolar biparietal Cardioblate Gemini-S (Medtronic) RF ablation system by performing two clamp lesions from the right and left sides that overlap in the middle of the posterior wall.**"。追加病変 = "circular lesion of the SVC, linear lesion connecting both caval veins and mitral isthmus line. ... in patients with prior history or intraprocedural induction of CTI-dependent flutter, the CTI is ablated endocardially."
    - ★**LAA（重要）**: "**Left atrial appendage exclusion should also be performed in all patients during minimally invasive surgical AF ablation.** Through an incision at the **third to fourth intercostal space** or via a completely thoracoscopic approach ... **Left atrial appendage exclusion must be performed at the very base of the structure, as to avoid leaving behind residual stump. Incomplete removal of the LAA is associated with increased risk of local thrombus formation and embolization.**"
    - ★**抗凝固不要という独自の適応**: "Since the thoracoscopic surgical AF ablation lesions are **exclusively epicardial**, one can perform the procedure **without the need for anticoagulation during and after the procedure**. Therefore, stand-alone thoracoscopic surgical AF ablation is **the best option for patients who have bleeding diathesis (particularly central nervous system bleeds)** ... Additional suitable patient subsets include those **without the ability to achieve LA access endocardially (large atrial septal occlusion device, interrupted inferior vena cava)** or those with **LAA thrombus**."

  - 前提: "**The LAA is the site of thrombus location in 90% of non-rheumatic AF patients with stroke**"。Cox maze III の初期評価で "a reduction in late stroke"、後続の後ろ向きシリーズで "a lower-than-expected incidence of late neurologic events after a Cox maze procedure, **independent of the preoperative CHA2DS2-VASc score or long-term warfarin use**"。**"The reduction in stroke has been attributed to both SR restoration and LAA elimination."**

## セクション横断の論点

1. **同じ根拠試験・異なる推奨クラス — 最大の矛盾**
   - **CTSN RCT（Gillinov NEJM 2015）と Cochrane 22 RCT（Huffman 2016）は5文書すべてが引用しているのに、結論は Class I（STS 僧帽弁・ESC 僧帽弁）／Class IIa（ESC 非僧帽弁）／Class 2a（ACC/AHA 全体）／Advice TO DO（EHRA/HRS）に割れる。**
   - **数値そのものも文書間で微妙に食い違う**: CTSN の1年 AF-free を **STS 2023 は 63% vs 29%、ACC/AHA 2023 は 63.2% vs 29.4%（P<0.001）、AATS 2026 は "roughly 70% at 1 year"** と記載。**AATS の "roughly 70%" は他2文書と整合しない → 要確認。**
   - → 統合レビューでは「ガイドラインが割れているのはエビデンスが割れているからではなく、**同じエビデンスに対する学会ごとの許容度の差**である」と明示すべき。とりわけ **ESC/EACTS が非僧帽弁を IIa に留め、STS が I/B-NR に置いた差**は、根拠文献リスト（ESC の 701, 703–707 は Cochrane＋観察研究群で RCT は pilot 1本のみ）を見れば、**STS 側がプール解析という自前の作業を追加したことに起因する**と読める。

2. **「アブレーションをやるか否か」は決着し、「どこまで焼くか」は決着していない**
   - AATS 2026 の Statement 3.4（**CoR I / LoE B-NR**）と 3.5（**CoR IIa / LoE C-expert opinion**）の格差が、この非対称を最も端的に示す。
   - lesion set に推奨を出しているのは **EHRA/HRS 2024（Advice TO DO / RAND）と AATS 2026（CoR IIa / C-EO）の2文書のみ**で、**ESC/EACTS と ACC/AHA は完全な空白**。ESC/EACTS は明示的に **STS 2017 ガイドライン（ref 681）に委ねている**。
   - → **「主要ガイドラインが Cox-Maze を推奨している」という記述は、ACC/AHA・ESC/EACTS からは支持されない**（ACC/AHA では "Cox-maze" が引用文献名にしか登場しない）。支持されるのは STS 2023（stand-alone の IIa/B-NR で "Cox maze III/IV lesion set as the preferred procedure"）、EHRA/HRS 2024、AATS 2026 の3文書のみ。

3. **PVI単独をどこまで否定するか — 3つの異なる線引き**
   - **STS 2023**: 「**LA ≥4.5 cm または中等度超MR**に対する PVI 単独」は Class III / LOE C（＝解剖学的・病変的な線引き）。
   - **AATS 2026**: 「**persistent / long-standing persistent AF**」への孤立性 PVI は "is not recommended"（＝AF病型による線引き）。さらに左房のみの ablation は「**75歳超・LA径>5cm・AF罹病5年超**」で劣る。
   - **EHRA/HRS 2024**: LA open 例では「biatrial Cox maze **または** 最低 PVI＋LA PWI」が必要（＝**最低ラインを PVI＋PWI に引く**）。closed 例では "should be individualized" と後退。
   - → **3文書とも「PVI単独では足りない」で一致するが、根拠となる閾値の軸が異なる（解剖 / AF病型 / 年齢＋LA径＋罹病期間）。統合レビューではこの3軸を並置して提示するのが正確。**
   - なお **ACC/AHA 2023 はカテーテル側で "PVI is recommended as the primary lesion set for all patients"（COR 1/A）とし、追加標的は "uncertain"（2b/B-R）** としている。**外科とカテーテルで PVI 単独の評価が正反対**である点は、統合レビューの重要な対比軸。

4. **後壁隔離（PWI）— 外科とカテーテルの評価が逆転する**
   - **カテーテル側**: CAPLA（338例、PVI 53.3% vs PVI+PWI 54.1%、差なし）、POBI-AF（217例、差なし）、Yu ら（113例、差なし）と**軒並み negative**。
   - **外科側**: Henn 2015 で **後壁未隔離 5年 33% vs 隔離 66%（P=0.017）**、僧帽弁例では**唯一の独立予測因子で 6.7倍**。STS 2023 も「後壁隔離の省略・不完全化」を失敗因子として名指し。
   - **EHRA/HRS 2024 自身がこの非対称の理由を提示している**: カテーテルでは LAPW の durable isolation が達成できない（**reconnection 40–100%**、septopulmonary bundle の厚み、心外膜脂肪、食道回避のための出力低下）。
   - → **「後壁は無意味」ではなく「隔離線が非透壁・再結合すれば無意味」と読むべき**という S6 の読みと完全に一致する。**外科がクランプで transmural な box を作れることが、後壁隔離の価値を決める。**

5. **transmurality は「デバイス」ではなく「反復回数」— 実験データが一貫している**
   - EHRA/HRS 2024 のみが提示: bipolar clamp 単発 **65%** → 2回連続（unclamp せず）**100%**／irrigated bipolar 単発 **74%** → 2回 **92%**／suction-assisted **68%**／EPi-Sense **15–100%（動物実験で変動大）**／cryoICE 心内膜 **98%**、CryoFlex **93%（LAA・PV）/ 84%（Cox maze線状）**。
   - "In a chronic animal study using this device with a single application, all lesions produced were transmural. However, **in clinical experience, multiple applications are needed to achieve exit block.**"
   - → **「1回クランプで transmural」は動物実験の話であり、ヒト心房＋心外膜脂肪では成立しない。**この一節は、Henn 2015 の「後壁を全部隔離できなかった群」がなぜ生じるかの機序的説明にもなっている。**手技論として最も実用的な一次データはこの文書にしかない。**

6. **LAA閉鎖後に抗凝固を止めてよいか — 学会間で正面から対立**
   - **ESC/EACTS 2024**: **Class I / Level C** で「rhythm outcome や LAA exclusion の有無に**関わらず** OAC を継続せよ」＝**明示的に中止を否定**。
   - **ACC/AHA 2023**: 抗凝固なしでの利益は **COR 2b / LOE A（uncertain）** ＝判断保留。
   - **AATS 2026**: **CoR IIa / LoE C-LD** で「**heart team の裁量・shared decision making**」＝実質的に中止を選択肢として認める。
   - **STS 2023**: 推奨は出さず、本文で「一部施設は SA+LAAO 後に単剤抗血小板のみで **stroke rate <1%**」を紹介（ref 184）。
   - **EHRA/HRS 2024**: AtriClip 291例で OAC 中止群の **87.5% 相対リスク減**（★**対照は実測群ではなく expected rate**）。
   - → **LAAOS III が「OAC 併用下の上乗せ効果」しか示していない（77% が OAC 継続）ことは全文書が認めているのに、そこからの実務的結論が真逆に分かれている。**進行中の **ATLAAC・LEAAPS・OCEAN/ALONE-AF（カテーテル）** が決着させる論点として統合レビューに立てるべき。

7. **LAA閉鎖の「手技の質」が Class 1 / LOE A で要求されるようになった — 手技論の格上げ**
   - **ACC/AHA 2023 の COR 1 / LOE A**: "**absence of flow across the suture line and a stump of <1 cm**"、**術中TEEで確認**。
   - **AATS 2026 の CoR I / LoE A（3.7）**: "reliable technique and **verified at the time of operation to be complete**"、判定基準は "**the lack of color Doppler flow into the LAA and collapsed LAA cavity**"、不完全なら **suture reinforcement**（クリップの付け直しは LAA 損傷リスク）。
   - **EHRA/HRS 2024**: "**at the very base**"、"**Incomplete removal of the LAA is associated with increased risk of local thrombus formation and embolization**"、**internal ligation・stapled excision/exclusion は late follow-up で成績不良と断言**。
   - **STS 2023**: "**Stapling only has had poor outcomes, with most patients having a residual stump and recanalization**"、クリップの誤配置で**回旋枝の圧迫**リスク。
   - 数値: クリップの完全閉鎖率は **>95%（ACC/AHA, Toale systematic review）／95%（ESC, van Laar 222例）／98%（AATS, EXCLUDE）／∼95-98%（AATS, 3研究）／60 of 61（EHRA, EXCLUDE 3か月）／291例で3年時全例（EHRA）**。不完全閉鎖時の塞栓イベントは **約25%（AATS, double-layer suture の2小研究）**。
   - ⚠**ただし AATS 2026 の LoE A は、「術中TEEでの完全性確認」自体を RCT で検証した研究が本文中に示されておらず（LAAOS III でも TEE は "recommended, but not mandated"）、根拠が読み取りにくい。**さらに **AtriCure と関係を持つ執筆者が複数いる点を COI として付記すべき。**

8. **POAF を減らすことに意味があるのか — STS 2026 が公式に留保した**
   - **"Current evidence suggests POAF may function more as a marker of perioperative or patient vulnerability than as a direct causal mechanism for adverse outcomes."**
   - これは、① 予防的アブレーション／LAAO を無格付けにした判断、② POAF後の抗凝固を IIb に下げた判断（stroke aOR 0.87 で減らず、出血 aOR 4.30、死亡 aOR 1.20）、の両方の論理的基盤になっている。
   - → **ESC/EACTS 2024 が「POAF は 4–5倍の再発AF・脳卒中/MI/心不全/死亡のリスク因子」と述べ、POAF後OACを IIa に格上げしたのと、STS 2026 の立場は正面から対立する。同じ疾患概念に対する因果性の解釈が学会間で割れている。**

9. **posterior pericardiotomy — 3文書で推奨されるが「誰に」が食い違う**
   - **ESC/EACTS 2024**: **無限定**（Class IIa / B）。
   - **ACC/AHA 2023**: **CABG / AVR / 上行大動脈瘤に限定**（根拠試験に僧帽弁・三尖弁が含まれないため）。
   - **STS 2026**: ESC 側（無限定）に同調しつつ、**"in patients without prior cardiac surgery" という別の限定を追加**。「isolated mitral valve surgery でのエビデンスはより限定的」とも明記。
   - **STS 2026 自身がこの差を明示的に記述している**（"the ACC/AHA guideline does not extend the recommendation to mitral or tricuspid surgery, whereas the ESC/EACTS guideline provides an unqualified endorsement"）。
   - → **僧帽弁手術＋Maze を行う症例に PP を追加すべきかは、3文書のどれからも直接には答えが出ない。**

10. **PFA は6文書すべての射程外 — 「PFA 以前の到達点」として位置づけるしかない**
    - STS 2023（検索期限 2023年1月）、ACC/AHA 2023（承認 2023年8月）、STS 2026（検索期限 2024年12月）はいずれも**外科PFAをゼロ言及**。ESC/EACTS 2024 はカテーテルPFAの非劣性RCT（607例）を1箇所引くのみ、EHRA/HRS 2024 は hybrid の endocardial 側について**将来形の1文**。
    - → **S1 の6文書は、外科PFAを扱うセクションに対しては「空白の一次資料」であり、「2024年時点でどの学会もPFAに推奨を与えていない」というネガティブな事実そのものが引用可能な所見である。**

11. **AFMR は STS 2023 と AATS 2026 の2文書しか扱わず、しかも両者の位置づけが異なる**
    - **STS 2023**: AFMR を「TAVR/TEER 検討例への外科提案（COR IIa / B-NR）」の**論拠**として概念的に用いる。**AFMR 特異的な数値エビデンスは一切提示していない。**
    - **AATS 2026**: AFMR を**主題**とし18ステートメントを提示。ただし "**no studies directly compare the relative effectiveness of PVI, left atrial maze, and biatrial maze specifically in the setting of AFMR**" と自認し、推奨はすべて**変性MRからの外挿＋病態論理**。
    - **ESC/EACTS 2024 と EHRA/HRS 2024 は完全な空白**（後者は "regurgitation" が文書全体で0回）。
    - ★**AFMR 特有の重要事実（AATS 2026 のみ）**: **AFMR での外科アブレーションは変性MRより成功率が低く、5年で 40–60% が AF 再発する。** そして **AF アブレーションを行っても遠隔期 TR は進行する**（"despite concomitant surgical ablation"／"even when treated with surgical atrial fibrillation ablation"）。MVP+Cox-Maze の前向き314例で **5年 freedom from ≥3+ TR は約50%**、TVP は **58.3%** に必要だった。
    - → **AFMR は「Maze が最も効きにくい基質（平均77歳・LA径5.1cm）でありながら biatrial maze が推奨される」という緊張関係を抱えており、PPI 11–15% を許容できるかのトレードオフの定量的評価は、どの文書にも存在しない。**

12. **S6（左房サイズ・左房縮小術）との接続**
    - **左房縮小術に推奨クラスを与えた文書は6編中ゼロ。** STS 2023 は "remains controversial and without strong evidence"、ACC/AHA 2023 は Table 3 に "may ↑ rates of sinus rhythm" の一行（**COR/LOE なし**）、ESC/EACTS・EHRA/HRS・AATS・STS 2026 は**記載なし**。→ **S6 で確認した「主要ガイドラインは LA reduction に言及していない」という Baudo の指摘（二次引用）は、S1 の一次資料6編で直接裏づけられた。**
    - **LA径のカットオフを推奨に書き込んでいるのは STS 2023 の "LA ≥4.5 cm"（PVI単独の禁止という文脈でのみ）が唯一。** AATS 2026 は "left atrial diameter >5 cm" を left-atrial-only ablation が劣る条件として挙げる（二次引用、ref 95 = Ad 2017）。→ **S6 で整理した 5.5 cm / 6.0 cm / 50 mm / 61.3 mm というカットオフ群と、ガイドラインの 4.5 cm / 5 cm はいずれも AP径ベースで、相互に検証されていない。LAVI に翻訳された閾値はガイドラインにも存在しない。**
    - **ESC/EACTS 2024 は2020年版の推奨文にあった「left atrial dilatation, years in AF, age, renal dysfunction」というリスク因子の列挙を2024年版で削除**しており、**患者選択の操作的定義がむしろ後退している**。S6 の結論（「LA径ではなく AF罹病期間が予測因子」Ad 2014: OR 1.13/年, 95%CI 1.05–1.22, P<.001）に照らすと、この削除は残念な後退である。

13. **抽出上の疑義・誤植の集約（統合レビューで孫引き禁止）**
    - **STS 2023**: Friedman ref 147 の "HR 0.88; 95% CI, 0.79-0.55"（下限>上限）／Samaras ref 171 の "OR 2.04; 95% CI, 1.03-1.22"（点推定値がCI外）／Patrick ref 89 の "HR 0.63; 95% CI, 0.039-1.00"（下限の桁異常）。引用番号のズレ（HARTCAP-AF に "24,134"、ハイブリッド系統的レビューに ref 133）。
    - **ESC/EACTS 2024**: "meta-analysis of three RCTs（IRR 0.55）" に付された引用番号669（FAST長期論文）が不自然（本文 pdf_text 6527–6529行、原文は "A meta-analysis of three RCTs confirmed a lower rate of atrial arrhythmia recurrence after thoracoscopic vs. catheter ablation (incidence rate ratio, 0.55; 95% CI, 0.38–0.78; with no heterogeneity between trials).669"）。2020側の Class/Level は表内並び順に依拠した読みで断定不可。
    - **EHRA/HRS 2024**: 推奨No.7 の Type of evidence（1308–1318）が内容と不整合 → **採用しないこと**。CASA-AF の "arrhythmia burden reduction of 75%" は不等号欠落で "≥75%" の可能性 → **要一次確認**。
    - **ACC/AHA 2023**: Table 2（COR/LOE定義表）が画像組みで抽出不能。**Section 8.1 の推奨2・5 の COR/LOE は確定できず採用不可**。CONVERGE の "0.0% versus 7.8%" の群順序が文意と逆。抗凝固 ≥3か月の一文が文法破綻。
