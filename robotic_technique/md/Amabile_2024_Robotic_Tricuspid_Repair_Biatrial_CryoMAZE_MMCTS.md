We describe in detail our technique for totally endoscopic, robotic-assisted tricuspid valve repair for iatrogenic tricuspid regurgitation and biatrial cryoMAZE.

Iatrogenic trauma to the tricuspid valve following pacemaker (PM) lead extraction is a known cause of significant tricuspid regurgitation (TR) \[1,2\]. The robotic platform is increasingly utilized for cardiac operations due to smaller incisions, improved visualization and enhanced dexterity \[3\]. We present a case of totally endoscopic, robotic-assisted tricuspid valve repair due to severe TR secondary to PM lead extraction.

- 1 - Patient presentation (0:12)
	We present the case of a 66-year-old male with sick sinus syndrome, atrial fibrillation and a recent history of right ventricular PM lead extraction after which he experienced progressive fatigue and decreased endurance. He was referred to our institution for isolated, iatrogenic, severe TR due to a torn septal tricuspid leaflet.
- 2 - Introduction (0:40)
	Robotic ports were placed as described previously \[4\], and cardiopulmonary bypass was achieved by percutaneous femoral artery and internal jugular vein cannulation \[5\]. Aortic cross-clamp was performed with the Chitwood endothoracic clamp. After snaring of the vena cava, the left atrium was accessed via Waterston’s groove.
- 3 - Left-sided CryoMAZE (1:21)
	We recognized and closed the left atrial appendage, which we routinely do from inside the atrium not only to achieve haemostatic occlusion of the appendage (which is also possible in an epicardial fashion, as previously reported \[6\]), but also to optimize visualization of the mitral valvular plane. We then performed left-sided CryoMAZE. We used a 2-minute freezing time on the epicardial coronary sinus line, mitral valve line, pulmonary vein line and base of the atrial appendix. After closure of the left atriotomy, a right atriotomy was performed, and the tricuspid valve was accessed and inspected.
- 4 - Tricuspid valve repair (2:00)
	Inspection of the tricuspid valve showed a torn septal leaflet. The tricuspid valve was repaired by reimplanting the tip of the leaflet to a papillary muscle-like structure on the right ventricular wall, closure of multiple clefts and placement of a 34-mm annuloplasty band. We then performed right-sided CryoMAZE.
- 5 - Right-sided CryoMAZE and outcome (6:46)
	We used a 2-minute freezing time on the isthmus line, the superior and inferior venae cavae and the right atrial appendage. At the end of the procedure, transoesophageal echocardiography revealed trace TR and a mean gradient of 1 mmHg. The patient was discharged home with normal sinus rhythm.

Our case demonstrates the safety and effectiveness of a totally endoscopic, robotic-assisted approach for the treatment of iatrogenic, severe TR and atrial fibrillation.

**Case Report Follow-up**

The patient was stable, asymptomatic and in normal sinus rhythm at the 6-month follow-up examination.
